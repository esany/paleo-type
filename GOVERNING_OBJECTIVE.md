# Governing objective

## Purpose

`paleo-type` exists to support scholarly excellent, evidence-constrained and practically usable critical study and unlocking of early-modern historical sources, especially handwritten manuscript material, as a **transdisciplinary research problem**.

The scholarly result and source understanding are the product. Data structures, software, AI, automation, GitHub workflow and presentation are replaceable support infrastructure.

## Governing invariants

1. **Evidence before plausibility.** Source observation, reading, normalization, identification and interpretation remain distinguishable. Uncertainty is a valid result; metadata, context, standards or AI output do not become source evidence by plausibility alone.
2. **Transdisciplinary source/problem orientation.** The research question and source determine which scholarly domains, methods and expertise are required. No current pilot, discipline, standard, model or technical architecture is universally sufficient by default; relevant disciplinary requirements must be activated together where the research problem demands it.
3. **Current expertise without standard fetishism.** Consequential method and architecture decisions use then-current authoritative standards, disciplinary practice, empirical evidence and relevant experiential specialist knowledge according to their actual evidential role. Contested choices remain explicit choices.
4. **Human-in-the-loop and accountability.** AI may provide proactive expert assistance, analysis and state maintenance, but AI-assisted method compliance is not independent expert validation. The non-specialist project owner controls research purpose, priorities, access/rights facts and consequential use/publication decisions and is not a substitute specialist adjudicator. Qualified specialists remain an escalation/validation layer where consequence or unresolved specialist judgement warrants it.
5. **Human auditability from result to evidence.** A non-specialist owner must be able to understand the active source/result, uncertainty, evidence basis, validation state and next meaningful action. A qualified reviewer must be able to trace consequential claims through provenance and decision history back to the exact source evidence at an appropriate level of detail. Human-readable views must not become parallel manually maintained truth stores.
6. **Technical subsidiarity and lean structure.** Use the least complex representation, workflow and tooling that preserve required scholarly distinctions, provenance, reproducibility, Human-in-the-loop and usability. Complexity, throughput, automation, corpus scale, schema completeness or standards compliance are never governing success criteria by themselves.
7. **Canonical and restartable state.** Consequential durable state must be recoverable from repository plus accessible/controlled source material without old chat. One fact/decision/state has one canonical owner; derived views, indexes and interfaces remain distinguishable from canonical research truth.

## Precedence

Derived layers follow this order:

`GOVERNING OBJECTIVE → scholarly/research requirements → disciplinary/epistemic requirements → methodology and research gates → corpus/project information model → Human-in-the-loop and validation workflow → software, AI tooling, automation, GitHub workflow and presentation`

Lower layers may implement or refine higher layers. They may not redefine or weaken them for convenience.

If methodology, data model, AI optimization, automation, GitHub workflow, project convention or implementation convenience conflicts with this governing scholarly objective, this objective prevails.

## Change authority

This objective may be analysed, challenged and amended by proposal, but a material change to its purpose, precedence or governing invariants requires an explicit decision by the human repository/project owner.

AI or automation must not infer permission to weaken or supersede this objective from implementation convenience, standards updates, repeated model agreement, prior autonomous changes or architecture-review results.

A material amendment must be visible and deliberate, with its rationale and consequences preserved in repository history. Routine research and maintenance work must not inherit unnecessary approval ceremony.
