# LEARNINGS — Reg. Ii 3

Status: project-specific lessons from source/context reconciliation through 2026-09-07. Canonical current state remains in `STATUS.md`; Source/File/crosswalk truth remains in `corpus/`.

## L001 — a whole archival file must not be collapsed into one DigiRef/provider source group

Reg. Ii 3 is a 525-leaf archival unit dated 1529. The currently modelled Neustadt and Gössitz/Knau income groups are bounded components inside it, alongside many other groups including Ziegenrück income material and the *Recesse der Visitatoren 1529*.

Corrective practice: keep archival-unit identity, bounded source-group identity and research locus separate. A source-group title/extent/date does not become the title/extent/date of the whole file.

Generalizable: yes.

## L002 — exact date evidence belongs to the object for which it is actually documented

The Neustadt group has an inspected DigiRef visitation period of 23.5.–1.6.1529. The Gössitz/Knau income group has no separately evidenced exact interval in the inspected DigiRef snapshot; it is securely situated inside the Reg. Ii 3 unit dated 1529, but the Neustadt interval must not be transferred to it.

Corrective practice: attach dates to an explicit target/context and preserve uncertainty/precision. Parent-unit dating is context, not an automatic child date.

Generalizable: yes.

## L003 — 1529 must not be called the first Neustadt visitation without qualification

A separate official finding-aid record documents visitation activity in Weida, Neustadt, Saalfeld and Pößneck already in 1527.

Corrective practice: use ordinal labels such as `first` only when the historical scope and evidence make the meaning explicit.

Generalizable: yes.

## L004 — intentionally partial research is a legitimate state

The Project has known internal Sources and exact File provenance, but no current owner-selected manuscript task. This is not a defect requiring symmetrical transcription merely because Reg. Ii 4 has progressed farther.

Corrective practice: distinguish identity/provenance maturity, scholarly processing depth and current research activity. Do not manufacture work to make Projects look equally complete.

Generalizable: yes.

## L005 — search/index hits are navigation evidence, not documentary boundaries

DigiRef place search normally exposes an entry point, often the first page of a larger place block. A place hit can support presence/context but cannot by itself establish the full source-group extent or documentary identity.

Corrective practice: use provider index/search evidence as the evidence class it is; verify documentary boundaries from catalogue/detail/source evidence.

Generalizable: yes.
