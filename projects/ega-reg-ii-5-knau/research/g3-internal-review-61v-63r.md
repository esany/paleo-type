3. **Unresolved accounting notation is legitimate.** Internal repertoire comparison does not yet yield exact values for the 61v left-side signs, so the current conservative sign representation should stand.
4. **Date wording remains wording.** Repetition strengthens the written `agustini` form, but neither the wording nor `xlvij` is converted here into an event date, archival date, production date or absolute year.
5. **The large 62v unresolved areas are not a representation failure.** They are source-located and reviewable; no new transcription schema is required merely because internal evidence currently fails to resolve them.
