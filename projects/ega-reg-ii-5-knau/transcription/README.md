# Transcription directory — current ownership

The current Reg. Ii 5 diplomatic witness state and the preserved historical transcription material coexist here with different authority.

- `first-reading/61v.tsv`, `62r.tsv`, `62v.tsv`, `63r.tsv` are the current canonical diplomatic owners for the completed fol. 61v–63r Editionsbaustein.
- `derived/61v-63r-first-reading.md` is a rebuildable human-readable view only; do not edit it to change manuscript truth.
- legacy `132.tsv`–`135.tsv` are verification/recovery evidence with unresolved or potentially mixed witness attribution. Their authority and promotion limits are owned by `../research/legacy-witness-provenance.tsv`.

Do not infer current authority from proximity, filename, export-image numbering or textual similarity. A legacy reading may inform a test, but any promoted Reg. Ii 5 reading must be supported by the exact target witness and persisted in `first-reading/`.

The current Project lifecycle and restart state are owned by `../STATUS.md`.
