# Apparatus directory — current role

This directory preserves historical decision/recovery material. It is **not** a current Reg. Ii 5 diplomatic or editorial decision owner.

`decisions.tsv` records earlier adjudications, rejected readings and uncertainty from work phases whose witness attribution may be unresolved or potentially mixed. Its current authority and promotion limits are owned by `../research/legacy-witness-provenance.tsv`.

A historical apparatus row may be used to recover a prior hypothesis, failure mode or decision trail. It may not by itself establish a current Reg. Ii 5 reading. Any reading that is to regain current witness authority must be re-established against the exact target Source/File/locus and persisted in the current canonical owner.

Current Reg. Ii 5 diplomatic readings are owned by `../transcription/first-reading/*.tsv`; bounded current analytical records live under `../research/` according to their stated role. Do not extend `decisions.tsv` as a current apparatus merely because a new comparison resembles an old case.
