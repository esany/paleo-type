Before any comparison wording is entered:

1. verify the exact comparison Source/File/locus and the evidence actually inspected;
2. ensure each witness is independently represented/read sufficiently for the intended collation claim;
3. map corresponding units explicitly rather than assuming line equivalence from similar wording;
4. classify agreements, variants, additions, omissions, order/structural differences, numerals/measures and source phenomena without collapsing witness identity;
5. preserve each witness's independent first-reading/G3 audit trail;

7. use legacy mixed-witness material only where its exact Source/File/locus provenance is adequate for the specific comparison claim;
8. never infer same hand, same date, direct copying or chronological priority from textual agreement alone.
