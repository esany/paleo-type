## What this project demonstrated

1. **Witness independence can be operationalized without folio isolation.** Independent first reading means no imported wording from a parallel witness. Exact, directly inspected same-witness comparanda may still be used to test graphical features after a target problem is observed.

2. **A bounded source-exact workflow can reach a meaningful stopping point with unresolved readings still visible.** G2 completeness, internal adjudication and controlled collation do not require every difficult token or sign to be resolved.

3. **External comparison is useful only after the principal witness is independently stable.** Reg. Ii 5 clarified correspondence and documentary selection but did not justify any Reg. Ii 4 diplomatic correction.

4. **Textual similarity is not hand identity.** Same record/source context and shared wording never established same hand; no Same-Hand claim was made.

5. **Selective correspondence is a stronger and safer result than a premature copy claim.** The demonstrated Reg. Ii 4 ↔ Reg. Ii 5 relation includes shared sequences, omissions, condensation and resegmentation. Direct copying mechanics and chronological priority remain open.

6. **Accounting/measure/sign systems deserve their own escalation path.** Alphabetic reading competence is insufficient for consequential values/units where the sign system remains uncertain.

7. **A completed Project task can still contain scientifically legitimate unresolved areas.** Current specialist-trigger areas include accounting/measure systems, the 306v-19 cancellation/flourish ambiguity and heavily obscured 309r material.
