# SD-KA-01 problem

The repository's scholarly/source contracts are comparatively disciplined, while the engineering-development surface still requires chronology-heavy reconstruction of current configuration, material findings, decision rationale, applicability and bounded work state. `requirements/` is a mixed brownfield namespace and local technical PASS can be mistaken for whole-system closure.

WP1 addresses the smallest first implementation: establish stable semantic owners and deterministic structural checks in place over representative current/legacy artifacts. It does not claim to solve scholarly acceptance, specialist validation or physical information-architecture migration.
