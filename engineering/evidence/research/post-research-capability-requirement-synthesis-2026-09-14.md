## 1. Executive disposition

The Research evidence does **not** justify one missing `Research Framing` layer, one master `activation` problem, one Candidate-Question object, one scoring model, one fixed competence topology, one preferred AI allocation or one software control-plane architecture.

The stronger synthesis result is narrower:

1. **Most high-value boundaries exposed by the Research are already normatively present.** The repository already requires explicit research questions, question/evidence/method alignment, source-/problem-relative disciplinary activation, uncertainty preservation, source-critical interpretation, Human-Owner purpose/priority authority, semantic-boundary protection, target-authority checks, no automatic `NEXT ACTION`, one canonical owner and technical subsidiarity.
2. **Several demonstrated failures are efficacy/mechanism failures, not missing normativity.** #165, #169, #162 and #90 remain materially different mechanisms and must not be recompressed into one label.
3. **The cross-source adaptive practices from #185/#186 mostly constrain how existing METHOD is applied rather than justify new system objects.** Analytical scale and context exposure must remain question/claim/evidence dependent; epistemic independence is not organizational isolation; transdisciplinary interaction can be constitutive.
4. **#191 closes the empirical absence-of-demonstration for plural serious question formation.** A rich state can support several serious candidate directions across heterogeneous source types. The recurring operations are thin: consequence-sensitive relation finding, question-dependent scale/context adjustment, discriminating-evidence identification, source-critical reformulation, explicit evidence ceilings and preservation of alternatives until priority authority acts.
5. **One residual generic need remains sufficiently evidenced to deserve a separate Concept-stage comparison:** when a rich research state does not already contain an owner-selected next scope, the research system should be able to support the formation and scholarly challenge of multiple serious evidence-grounded research directions **without converting them into task authority** and without treating addressable residuals as priority merely because they are unresolved.
6. That residual need is **not yet evidence for a new persistent Capability object or new architecture**. The next stage, if admitted, must determine whether the smallest adequate response is no new norm, a refinement/operationalization of existing Research-question control, or another bounded mechanism. This synthesis does not choose the form.

No implementation is justified by this synthesis alone.

## 2. Canonical baseline already carrying substantial coverage

### 2.1 Governing Objective

The Governing Objective already requires:

- evidence before plausibility;
- transdisciplinary source/problem orientation;
- question/source-determined expertise rather than universal discipline/architecture;
- Human Owner control of purpose, priorities and consequential use;
- qualified specialist review where consequence or unresolved judgement warrants it;
- technical subsidiarity and lean structure;
- canonical/restartable state.

These are not new Requirements created by #184–#191.

### 2.2 Authoritative scholarly workflow

`methodology/historical-source-workflow.md` already owns explicit **Research-question control**:

```text
research goal
→ research question
→ evidence needed
→ disciplinary method
→ claim type
→ permitted conclusion
```

It also requires:

- material work to serve an explicit question or required source-representation task;
- evidence capable of answering the question;
- the discipline/method governing the inference;
- explicit stronger unsupported claims;
- problem-relative activation of specialist domains;
- source criticism before historical interpretation;
- depth proportional to source and research question;
- uncertainty to remain unresolved when evidence does not decide.

The Research therefore does not establish that question/evidence/method control is absent from METHOD.

### 2.3 Project and priority authority

`methodology/project-lifecycle.md`, `AGENTS.md` and `methodology/workflow.md` already separate:

```text
Project authority
Source/File identity
Project structural status
Project→Source research function
current work target / NEXT ACTION
```

A residual uncertainty, Source role, available File, support task, gate state, technical readiness or GitHub artefact does not select scholarly priority.

A Project may validly have no owner-selected `NEXT ACTION`.

This already carries the most important authority boundary exposed by post-#166 and #191:

```text
candidate research direction != authorized scope
```

### 2.4 Semantic / cross-boundary protection

`AGENTS.md`, D-019 and `methodology/workflow.md` already require claim type, target/scope, evidence/authority/review level and uncertainty to survive consequential translations across scholarly, technical and human-language boundaries.

This is direct existing coverage for the failure family exemplified by #165 and #169. Research support is not a reason to duplicate the rule.

### 2.5 Quality and anti-infrastructure controls

`methodology/quality-and-learning.md` already asks about evidence, layer separation, independence, persistence and goal alignment, and requires tool/workflow proposals to show demonstrated need, smallest adequate mechanism and no speculative infrastructure.

`AGENTS.md` already requires Research outcome before infrastructure.

Therefore a successful synthesis must prefer reuse/refinement over a new process layer.

## 6. Residual Requirement / reusable-system candidate

### 6.1 Demonstrated need

When a Project or comparable research state is sufficiently rich to support several serious directions and no owner-selected next scope already exists, the system should be able to support scholarly formation and challenge of **multiple evidence-grounded candidate research directions** before priority selection.

The need exists because:

- #191 demonstrates that multiple serious directions can be formed across three heterogeneous fixtures;
- stronger directions arise from consequential relations/patterns rather than addressable residuals alone;
- serious directions can name evidence that would discriminate/advance them and legitimate evidence ceilings;
- source criticism can materially reformulate or reject the question;
- substantive questions remain source-/purpose-sensitive;
- Human Owner authority must remain the selector of material priority/scope.

### 6.2 Boundary of the candidate

The candidate must **not** imply:

- automatic next-task selection;
- a priority score;
- a mandatory Candidate-Question schema;
- persistent question objects;
- a fixed stage sequence;
- a fixed competence leadership table;
- a preferred AI role;
- a Human-Owner UI;
- a workflow engine;
- an assumption that every terminal research state requires a new question.

A legitimate result remains:

```text
no further scope selected
```

### 6.3 Best current abstraction level

The evidence supports only the following pre-normative reusable property:

> **When research-direction selection is actually needed, the system should preserve and make reviewable more than one serious source-/purpose-grounded direction where the evidence supports plurality, together with enough scholarly basis to distinguish consequential questions from merely addressable residuals, while leaving priority/scope authority with the Human Owner.**

This is a **Requirement/refinement candidate**, not yet a new REQ-ID or Capability block.

### 6.4 Why this is not already fully discharged

Existing METHOD controls the quality and authority of a selected research question very well. Existing Project rules also correctly permit no selected next action.

What is less explicit is the transition:

```text
rich completed state
+ no selected next scope
+ owner/purpose requires a priority decision
→ several serious scholarly directions can be surfaced and challenged
→ none acquires task authority before owner selection
```

The gap is therefore not `no research question control`; it is the weaker operational/normative visibility of **plural pre-priority scholarly direction formation and challenge**.

### 6.5 Confidence / evidence ceiling

Confidence is sufficient for a separate Concept-stage comparison because the positive recurrence now spans three materially different source structures.

Confidence is **not** sufficient to decide:

- a persistent representation;
- user interaction design;
- Human/AI allocation;
- automated ranking;
- software architecture;
- universal formalization.

Those remain downstream and trigger-specific.

## 9. Authority and judgement disposition

### Qualified specialist judgement remains irreducible where triggered

Includes contested archival/diplomatic, palaeographic, philological, editorial, metrological/accounting, historical/source-critical or other disciplinary judgement whose consequence warrants qualified review.

This synthesis does not convert specialist judgement into system logic.

### Human Owner judgement remains irreducible for

- research purpose;
- material priority and scope selection;
- whether a candidate direction becomes current Project work;
- material research-system implementation admission;
- consequential publication/use;
- Governing Objective amendment.

A generated or challenged candidate direction is not owner authority.

### Deterministic system control is appropriate only where semantics are settled

Examples remain identity/reference integrity, canonical-vs-derived boundaries, stable provenance constraints, generated views, migration/loss tests and bounded technical enforcement such as an already-settled promotion-control rule.

No evidence supports deterministic scoring of scholarly question value or machine selection of research priority.

Evidence-before-plausibility, claim/source/authority separation, question/evidence/method control, adaptive scale/context use, source criticism, explicit evidence ceilings, uncertainty, candidate-vs-authorized-scope separation, Human-Owner priority authority, specialist judgement boundaries, technical subsidiarity and canonical/restartable state.

- premise-depth/review efficacy (#171).

### Where is there a genuine normative or reusable-system need?

One bounded residual is sufficiently evidenced: **plural evidence-grounded research-direction formation/challenge before Human-Owner priority selection when a rich state and decision need actually require it.**

Whether this requires any new norm beyond a refinement/operationalization of existing Research-question control remains a Concept-stage question.

### Which #191 operations are system-relevant, if any?

System-relevant as constraints/properties rather than components:

- consequence-sensitive relation finding;
- question-dependent scale/context adjustment;
- discriminating evidence and evidence ceilings;
- source-critical reformulation;
- preserving multiple credible directions until priority authority acts.

Their substantive execution remains source-/purpose-/domain-sensitive.

Source-/domain-specific formation, challenge and adjudication of scholarly questions and claims where specialist competence is consequential.

### What remains Human-Owner judgement?

Purpose, material priority/scope, selection among candidate directions, material implementation admission and consequential use/publication.

### What can be deterministically enforced?

Only already-settled structural/technical semantics and bounded controls. No generic deterministic research-question ranking or priority selection is supported.

### Is any material research-system change justified now?

**No implementation or architecture change is justified directly by this synthesis.**
