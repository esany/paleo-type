## 3. Minimal evidence-derived programme capability map

The smallest useful capability set derived from the Governing Objective plus demonstrated evidence is seven programme-level capabilities. These are synthesis groupings, not new canonical objects or formal Requirements.

### PC1 — Evidence, provenance and semantic/authority integrity

**Intended outcome:** Consequential claims remain traceable to the evidence and semantic/authority layer that actually supports them; valid local facts do not silently acquire stronger scholarly, task or planning authority.

**Why required:** Governing Objective invariants 1, 4, 5 and 7; D-019; evidence/provenance and one-owner Requirements.

**Main P0/P1 evidence:** B3/A9; #165; #169; #184; #185; #186.

**Deliberately outside:** research-priority selection; fixed cross-domain ontology; global glossary.

### PC2 — Adaptive transdisciplinary inquiry and question-relative object/scale/context formation

**Intended outcome:** The research question/source activate the relevant expertise and allow analytical object, evidence depth, context exposure and scale to change when the claim/evidence requires it, without contamination or authority laundering.

**Why required:** Governing Objective invariant 2; source criticism; source-/problem-relative expertise routing.

**Main P0/P1 evidence:** A3/A4, B1/B2/B4, C4; RF185-C01–C06; RI186-03–07; RF191-C2/C4/C5.

**Deliberately outside:** fixed competence topology; universal context-first/context-last sequence; universal analysis-unit model.

### PC3 — Evidence-grounded research-direction formation and Human-Owner priority preparation

**Intended outcome:** When a material next-direction decision is needed, serious source-/purpose-grounded alternatives can be formed and challenged without addressability becoming priority and without candidates acquiring task authority.

**Why required:** Governing Objective invariants 2, 4 and 5; A1/A2/A5/B5; #191; D-020.

**Main P0/P1 evidence:** post-#166 bounded stop; RF191-C1/C3/C5; #198; #203–#205.

**Deliberately outside:** scoring, persistent Candidate-Question schema, automatic priority, fixed UI, preferred AI role.

### PC4 — Premise-depth challenge, consequence-scaled review and specialist validation

**Intended outcome:** Review tests the consequential premise/evidence at sufficient depth; fresh context is not mistaken for independent evidence; contested specialist judgement remains visibly distinct from AI/method compliance.

**Why required:** Governing Objective invariants 1, 3, 4 and 5; quality/falsification obligations.

**Main P0/P1 evidence:** D2; #171/RF184-P04; RI186-05; current `quality-and-learning.md`; REQ-AI-010 / REQ-UX-008 / REQ-UX-010 triggers.

**Deliberately outside:** permanent independent-review role or universal specialist panel.

### PC5 — Bounded execution, support subsidiarity and scientifically authorized task progression

**Intended outcome:** Once work is authorized, evidence/support/technical mechanics help execute it without manufacturing scope, blocker state or research priority; support effort does not become the product.

**Why required:** Governing Objective invariants 4 and 6; A8/A9/D1/D3; workflow target/dependency authority.

**Main P0/P1 evidence:** #169; post-#166; current `workflow.md`; REQ-RSE-001/009 and related anti-infrastructure rules.

**Deliberately outside:** programme-level priority selection and broad architecture.

### PC6 — Canonical/restartable continuity and function-preserving evolution

**Intended outcome:** A fresh context can reconstruct current state/cursor without old chat; canonical changes propagate to the right restart/navigation surfaces; simplification/migration does not silently discard material work-state or cognitive/review functions.

**Why required:** Governing Objective invariants 5 and 7; one-owner/restartability rules.

**Main P0/P1 evidence:** C2/C3/D1; #162 freshness history; #200; #208; REQ-GWP-008/010; maintenance bounded-consistency semantics.

**Deliberately outside:** mandatory programme registry/state machine/dashboard/Milestone.

### PC7 — Deterministic enforcement of settled structural/technical semantics

**Intended outcome:** Where meaning and required control are already settled, machine-checkable constraints prevent known deterministic failure classes without pretending to validate scholarship.

**Why required:** Governing Objective invariants 5–7; technical subsidiarity; REQ-AI-008 / RSE deterministic boundary.

**Main P0/P1 evidence:** #90/RF184-P08; existing source-contract pattern; #205's separation of scholarly assurance from missing branch checks.

The repository's bounded execution/restart machinery is strong, but that strength can faithfully preserve a locally valid arc while the broader problem landscape drops out of active planning. #208 demonstrates this at programme level.

**Disposition:** do not weaken bounded restartability; address the distinct programme/phase-continuity question through PC6b/#200.

Current METHOD keeps relevant expertise available and cross-source Research shows that interaction can be constitutive. Historical G2 demonstrates productive cross-domain consequence classification. However, no fixed orchestration mode is supported.

**Disposition:** PC2 remains `ADEQUATE NORM / EFFICACY OBSERVATION`; a permanent Domain Review role, competence registry or multi-agent topology is not warranted.

### 5.3 Contamination protection and productive context reactivation must remain claim-dependent

Reg. Ii 5, Darwin and Washington show that context can be contaminating, constitutive evidence or necessary aggregate structure depending on the claim.

**Disposition:** preserve adaptive current method; no universal context stage. Reopen only on a concrete failure or on a proposed mechanism that needs validation.

### 5.4 Fresh context is not sufficient premise-depth review

#171 shows that session/context freshness does not guarantee independent premise testing. Current quality semantics correctly define independence by evidence/premise checking rather than isolation.

**Disposition:** preserve and observe; no standing independent-review bureaucracy.

### 5.5 Lean correctness can coexist with function loss

Earlier simplification corrected false semantics while losing some work-state/audit function. The analogy to absorbed transdisciplinary role structures is plausible but not fully demonstrated as a generic current failure.

**Disposition:** trigger-based loss review on material simplification/migration; no new standing layer now.

### 5.6 Canonical-owner correctness does not guarantee planning/programme continuity

This is the strongest current composition failure. Canonical Project/METHOD owners can be individually correct while visible current planning/programme coverage becomes stale or incomplete.

**Disposition:** PC6b moves beyond observation to a **P2 Requirements-resolution candidate** because the problem has repeated and produced programme-level consequence. The solution form remains open.

### 5.7 Human-Owner authority does not itself supply scholarly decision support

D-020 now makes the pre-priority scholarly operation explicit without transferring priority authority. The repository still has no empirical HCI evidence for a fixed comparison/UI format.

**Disposition:** preserve D-020 and observe real use. A concrete UI/HCI route is triggered only if a proposed decision surface becomes consequential or real use demonstrates a recurring transition failure.

### 5.8 Technical/process correctness does not establish scientific scope correctness

This remains an explicit cross-cutting boundary. #207 itself is evidence that locally correct process closure must be composed with programme-level coverage before a programme-completion claim.

**Disposition:** PC6b/Programme synthesis is the relevant current response; no master QA flag.

### 5.9 #90 is real and orthogonal

## 7. Programme-level completion criteria

The research-system programme may be judged **sufficiently mature / no further current system work** when all of the following are true at the then-current evidence ceiling:

1. the programme-level capability set and its evidence/owners remain reconstructable without old chat;
2. evidence/provenance/semantic/authority boundaries have current owners and no known material unowned failure class;
3. adaptive transdisciplinary inquiry can proceed without a fixed topology and any demonstrated activation/context/scale failure has a responsible trigger/route;
4. pre-priority direction formation is either working adequately in real use or any demonstrated recurring post-D-020 mechanism failure is explicitly routed (for example through #202 if its trigger actually fires);
5. consequence-scaled review can reach the contested premise/evidence depth and specialist validation remains explicitly distinguishable from AI/method compliance;
6. bounded execution/support work remains subordinate to authorized scholarly targets;
7. **programme/phase continuity has a settled disposition**: either existing owners are shown sufficient, or the smallest justified positive lifecycle/feedback hook has been admitted, checked and result-reviewed; a local terminal state must not require old chat to distinguish it from programme completion;
8. known material lean-down/migration changes receive proportionate function/loss review; evidence-limited cognitive-function hypotheses may remain trigger-based when no current change requires them;
9. **settled canonical-promotion controls are actually enforced or explicitly re-dispositioned**; the current #90 gap cannot be silently treated as complete merely because Git history is recoverable;
10. known deferred/Project-/gate-specific Requirement gaps have responsible owners and explicit activation conditions rather than being hidden unfinished programme work;
11. future material research-system changes traverse the D-016 evidence→Requirements→Concept→owner admission→development→checks→Result Review chain, and their demonstrated consequences feed back into the programme view;
12. remaining uncertainty is compatible with the Governing Objective and does not require a speculative architecture merely to make the programme look complete.

Deferred triggers are compatible with programme completion. Hidden material gaps are not.
