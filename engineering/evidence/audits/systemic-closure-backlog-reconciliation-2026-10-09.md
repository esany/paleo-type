## Closure model

Each relevant Issue must end in exactly one disposition:

- `CLOSE`: own DoD proven.
- `SUPERSEDE`: remaining work transferred to a provenance-linked successor.
- `SPLIT`: completed and unresolved parts separated.
- `DEFER`: future trigger and entry condition recorded.
- `KEEP OPEN`: one concrete current unmet condition remains.
- `DUPLICATE`: canonical owner linked.
- `BLOCKED BY EVIDENCE` or `BLOCKED BY AUTHORITY`: exact blocker and next admissible condition recorded.

Comments are not counted as progress unless they add evidence, a decision, a durable state change or a provenance-preserving transition.
