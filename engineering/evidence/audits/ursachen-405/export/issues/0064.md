### 2. Where Line is not enough: source-evidenced need for finer target spans

The fixture does **not** justify making every word/token a first-class object. It does demonstrate that a consequential target sometimes occupies only part of a physical line:

- `61v-L16`: visible cancelled/struck word/word-initial unit inside the line;

Minimum information requirement: **canonical physical line + an unambiguous line-local extent/anchor for consequential sub-line phenomena/readings/decisions**. Span boundaries remain scholarly observations where ambiguous; target referential integrity can be deterministic after they are established.

### 3. Layout / reading-order loss

61v is empirically non-uniform: eight physical lines form a spatial list/accounting block in which left quantity/sign notation belongs to the corresponding written entry. The legacy `region=list` rows preserve a broad label but do not preserve the physical 8-line structure or a reliable sign↔entry/source-line relation. 62r/62v paragraph boundaries and the 63r continuation→heading→new-section boundary are source-relevant as well.

Minimum requirement: preserve **folio boundary, ordered physical lines, relevant block/section membership and the 61v local spatial association needed to understand the list**. Exact page coordinates, a universal layout ontology, or a separate marginal stream are not demonstrated requirements.

### 4. Confirmed source-phenomenon loss: cancellation/deletion

Minimum requirement: **source phenomenon and surviving/current reading must be independently representable at the same line/span**. The deleted reading may remain unresolved; representation completeness must not force its reconstruction.

### 5. Written abbreviation vs editorial expansion is currently collapsed/ad hoc

Checkpoint A observes recurrent abbreviation/contraction marks on all four folia. Legacy readings contain editorially expanded-looking forms inline, e.g. `It(em)` and `d(er)`, while an evidence note explicitly says abbreviation expansion is “not yet formalized”.

This demonstrates the REQ-PAL-005 / REQ-PHIL-002 / REQ-EDIT-003 risk: a written sign and editor-provided expansion cannot share one undifferentiated `reading` value if the diplomatic layer is to remain recoverable.

Minimum requirement: **written abbreviation/sign remains diplomatic source data; optional expansion is a distinct editorial value with its own status/evidence.** No expansion is required merely because an abbreviation is observed.

### 6. Numeral/accounting notation: partial support is already good; no separate ontology justified

### 7. Unresolved/variant history is valuable and must not be replaced by a new flat status vocabulary

The fixture actually uses:

- current reading assessment: `observed`, `working`, `variant`, `unresolved`;

No closed universal transition/status ontology is justified.
