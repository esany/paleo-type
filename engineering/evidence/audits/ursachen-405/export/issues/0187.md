## Scope

Persist the complete non-normative Research plan that was developed from the 2026-09-14 audit/problem-landscape work, without promoting it to METHOD, Requirements, Project priority, architecture or implementation.

## Why

The repository now has a rich Concept Candidate but still lacks the empirical evidence needed to decide which observed needs are persistent/generic and which solution ideas are actually justified. The plan therefore separates primary evidence collection from later secondary classification and Concept Challenge.

It explicitly protects against two symmetric risks:

- **Concept Bias** — the existing solution frame shaping the evidence that later appears to support it;
- **Decomposition Bias** — over-isolating disciplines and thereby removing scientifically necessary transdisciplinary interaction.

Working principle:

> independence without isolation — interaction without authority laundering
