
The mistake is not using small slices. The mistake is allowing **slice size to leak into the acceptance scope of the original problem**.

> **Lean determines the size of the next learning/delivery step, not the size of the problem that may be declared solved.**

> **Small iteration != small acceptance scope of the original Human need.**

### P4 — acceptance claim carries its tested envelope

A PASS/acceptance must remain bounded by the property, fixture/context, consequence class and evidence actually exercised. Stronger Generic-Fit requires materially appropriate falsification evidence.

### P5 — real regression causes acceptance re-evaluation

When a later real fixture reproduces a mechanism that a current acceptance claim materially implied was covered, the current owner must explicitly disposition the claim as one of:

`still covered at narrower scope | partial | re-entry required | residual platform/model limit | new distinct failure`.

Do not require historical Issues to reopen automatically; require the **current acceptance owner** to account for the new evidence.

### P6 — Lean is applied after sufficiency framing

Small slices are encouraged. They must be selected **after** the parent problem/necessary properties are understood enough that a slice cannot silently redefine the success criterion.

### P7 — vertical efficacy evidence is first-class

Where the problem is Human-facing composition, deterministic component regressions are necessary but insufficient. At least one real vertical fixture must test the composition that the acceptance claim actually makes.

### P8 — assurance ceiling is explicit

Where future model behavior cannot be mechanically guaranteed, state that limit. Do not convert `rule exists + tests pass` into `future execution cannot repeat the error`.
