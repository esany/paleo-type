## Status / authority

**P1 role-definition / governance fixture. No change to human authority is authorized by this Issue.**

This Issue makes the existing **Human Owner / Governance function** explicit so specialized AI work contexts can distinguish what must remain a human decision from what should be handled by scholarly or technical roles without owner micromanagement.

The role is not a software role and is not a substitute specialist function.

## Core purpose

Preserve human control over consequential purpose, authority and use while avoiding transfer of routine repository mechanics or specialist adjudication to a non-specialist owner.

Use:

```text
AI/scholarly/Dev work proceeds within canonical method
→ only genuinely owner-reserved choice reaches human owner
→ owner decides purpose/authority/admission/use
→ specialist or operational questions return to their proper roles
```

Not:

```text
AI is uncertain
→ ask owner to decide technical path or manuscript reading
```

## Human-owner authority

The human owner controls, where applicable:

- research purpose and priorities;
- whether a genuinely new project should exist and what research object/active scope it serves;
- source access only the owner can provide;
- rights/authority facts that cannot otherwise be verified;
- consequential publication/use decisions;
- explicit admission of material research-system changes under the canonical D-016 path;
- material amendments to `GOVERNING_OBJECTIVE.md`;
- other normative choices that cannot legitimately be derived from evidence, method and canonical state.

## What should not be delegated to the owner

The owner should not be required to decide:

- routine repository paths/owners/resolvers/validators when canonically derivable;
- ordinary implementation details inside an admitted scope;
- whether a visible grapheme is correct merely because AI is uncertain;
- specialist palaeographic/philological/archival/historical adjudication beyond the owner's expertise;
- deterministic consistency questions the system can derive/check;
- which canonical file/table owns a fact when repository rules already decide it;
- routine synchronization/state maintenance.

These remain Source Research, Domain Review, Dev or specialist responsibilities.

## Owner decision request contract

A request to the human owner should contain only the information necessary for the genuine owner decision:

```text
DECISION NEEDED
what the owner actually controls

WHY THIS IS OWNER-RESERVED
why evidence/method/role authority cannot decide it

CURRENT STATE
relevant canonical/research status

OPTIONS / CONSEQUENCES
only real alternatives, without technical noise

RECOMMENDATION
where the reviewing role can responsibly recommend one

WHAT IS NOT BEING ASKED
technical/specialist questions excluded from the owner decision

RESULTING NEXT ACTION
what follows after the owner's decision
```

Do not present routine engineering choices as governance decisions merely to obtain permission.

## Material change admission

For a material research-system change:

`analysis → requirements → concept → explicit owner admission → development → tests/loss checks → scholarly result review`.

Owner admission is about permission/scope. It does not:

- establish specialist truth;
- waive semantic-loss review;
- replace Domain Review;
- authorize unrelated scope expansion;
- make the owner responsible for implementation mechanics.

## Rights/access interface

When exact source access or rights facts cannot be inferred/verified, ask the owner for the narrow missing fact or access action.

After authority is established, routine provenance/repository synchronization returns to the relevant operational role.

## Publication/use interface

Consequential external publication/use remains a human decision.

Before that decision, Domain Review should present the result, uncertainty, evidence and validation status in ordinary research-facing language. The owner should not need to reverse-engineer implementation detail.

## Specialist boundary

Human accountability does not mean the non-specialist owner must personally validate specialist readings.

Where independent expert judgement is required:

`Domain Review → exact-source specialist packet → qualified independent specialist → result/validation state → Domain Review/owner as appropriate`.

The owner decides whether/when consequential use proceeds; the specialist adjudicates the specialist question.

## Challengeability / stop authority

The owner must be able to:

- inspect consequential results and their evidence basis;
- challenge or request explanation;
- stop or redirect research purpose/priorities;
- decline material implementation admission;
- decline consequential publication/use.

Operational AI ownership must remain explainable enough for this control without making the owner operate the repository as a software system.

## Model-independent procedural principle

Different AI sessions may make different recommendations. Stable elements should be:

- which choices are owner-reserved;
- the minimum decision packet;
- separation from specialist/technical questions;
- explicit admission boundaries;
- documented consequence/next action.

Do not manufacture owner approval from silence, enthusiasm or earlier autonomous work.

## Relationship to #71

This Issue is the Human Owner/Governance role fixture for the generic blueprint. It should primarily confirm/operationalize existing authority, not create new owner duties.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] owner-reserved decisions are explicit and consistent with Governing Objective/maintenance;
- [ ] routine technical and scholarly-specialist decisions are explicitly excluded;
- [ ] owner decision requests have a minimal reusable contract;
- [ ] material-change admission is clearly permission/scope, not scholarly validation;
- [ ] rights/access and publication/use boundaries are explicit;
- [ ] non-specialist owner is not used as substitute expert reviewer;
- [ ] owner retains challenge/stop authority without repo micromanagement;
- [ ] another capable AI can identify when owner input is genuinely required without old-chat instructions;
- [ ] no new governance ceremony is introduced for routine work.

## Non-goals

- no expansion of owner workload;
- no owner approval for every write/change;
- no technical micromanagement;
- no specialist validation by the owner;
- no change to Governing Objective;
- no workflow engine or approval system.

## Rights/access refinement from source-availability failure

The Reg. Ii 5 case shows that owner routing must distinguish **rights/authority facts** from **routine evidence-storage/access mechanics**.

### Owner-reserved questions

Ask the Human Owner only when the answer cannot be established from existing rights/access records or authoritative provider terms, for example:

- may the supplied source/file be retained in a controlled/private research environment?
- is public redistribution permitted?
- is access limited to institutional retrieval/use only?
- is there a project-purpose decision that affects whether the source should be retained/used?

### Not owner-reserved by default

Once the allowed access/storage class is established, do not ask the owner to choose technical mechanics such as repository path, local controlled working directory, derivative filename, checksum operation or render command when those are canonically/operationally derivable.

### Important non-equivalences

Do not infer:

`not publicly redistributable → must remain transient`

and do not infer:

`private repository → rights-compliant`.

The owner supplies/decides only the genuine authority fact where necessary; Source/Provenance/Dev roles implement the permitted access path.

### Human-visible source requirement

Where the research task requires manuscript inspection, the governance goal is not merely to preserve an identifier. The authorized research process must make the actual image available to the human/source-research reviewer under the permitted conditions.
