## Text

## Purpose

Run a bounded **Competence Availability / Knowledge Access Audit** against already canonical disciplinary requirements and real Reg. Ii 5 A–G research fixtures.

The problem is **not** missing competency modelling. `domain-requirements.tsv`, `GOVERNING_OBJECTIVE.md`, `AGENTS.md` and `methodology/historical-source-workflow.md` already define a broad transdisciplinary competency landscape and epistemic boundaries. The demonstrated gap is operational:

> when an already-identified discipline is needed, can the system actually retrieve, inspect, cite and turn qualified specialist knowledge into a better diagnostic question, comparator strategy or falsification test within a bounded research turn?

This Issue must therefore audit **knowledge access / expertise activation**, not create another domain map, ontology, knowledge base or methodology layer.

## Canonical premise

Existing requirements already cover, among others:

- archival science / diplomatics;
- palaeography / scribal evidence;
- historical philology / language history / lexicography;
- scholarly editing / textual criticism;
- historical source criticism;
- source-type/domain history;
- codicology/material manuscript study;
- onomastics/toponymy;
- historical metrology/numismatics/accounting notation;
- research-data stewardship / GWP;
- research software/data engineering;
- AI/HITL;
- human research usability/transparency.

Examples of already-canonical obligations include:

- `REQ-PHIL-005`: material lexical/semantic claims require the actually inspected dictionary/corpus/article entry or passage;
- `REQ-PAL-004`: palaeographic comparison must use discriminating graphical features rather than repeated word forms/silhouettes;
- `REQ-DOM-009`: tacit expertise should generate explicit questions/candidates/tests rather than invisible conclusions.

No new requirement family is presumed.

## Audit question

For a real expert move, distinguish the following operational levels without turning them into a new schema:

```text
known
→ findable
→ accessible
→ concretely inspected / citable
→ translatable into a diagnostic question or search strategy
```

A resource is not operational competence merely because a URL, title or handbook is known.

## Real fixtures

Use already-paid Reg. Ii 5 work rather than a generic review of every discipline for completeness.

### Retrospective A–F4

For material decisions already made in clusters A–F4, ask:

1. What exact scholarly decision/problem had to be solved?
2. Which specialist experiential knowledge would a capable domain expert likely activate?
3. Where could that knowledge be obtained reproducibly today?
4. Can it actually be reached and inspected inside a research turn?
5. Does it produce a better hypothesis, comparator, search strategy or falsification test?
6. Does it materially reduce the search space?
7. Can its contribution be traced to an actually inspected qualified source?
8. At what stage may it be activated without contaminating source-only observation?
9. Is the value one-off or plausibly reusable for similar cases?

Do not reopen settled readings merely to populate this audit.

### Prospective G fixture

Use current #123 cluster G (`63r-L04` display heading) as a prospective test **without letting external knowledge replace the target graph**.

After source-only target observation is frozen, test whether qualified knowledge access can improve the diagnostic search around, for example:

- historically plausible rubric/headword forms;
- comparable rubrics in visitation/administrative records;
- source-type/document-structure expectations as hypothesis generators only;
- contemporary/regional display-capital morphology;
- secure `Z`/`S`/other display-initial exemplars from qualified references;
- analogous headings in source collections or scholarly editions.

Success is not “a source says the word should be Zulage”. Success is that the activated expertise identifies better discriminating pixel features, better comparator classes or a smaller viable candidate set before target reinspection.

Cluster H may be used later only if the audit shows a specific need, especially for Roman-sign/notation expertise. No calendar/date normalization is authorized.

## Measures

For each expert move/resource path record qualitatively or, where practical, quantitatively:

- **knowledge latency** — time/steps from recognized specialist problem to usable information;
- **diagnostic leverage** — whether the information changes the next testable question;
- **search-space reduction** — whether it eliminates low-value paths or prioritizes a small set of viable tests;
- **traceability** — whether the contribution can be cited to an actually inspected passage/image/entry;
- **contamination risk** — when it may safely enter relative to blind/source-only observation and target return;
- **reuse value** — one-off versus recurring value for comparable problems.

The audit should prefer evidence of changed research behaviour over a catalogue of resources.

## Expected output

Produce a concise prioritized gap record of the form:

```text
expert move / fixture
→ governing existing requirement/domain
→ knowledge needed
→ current access state
→ best qualified source/tool path found
→ latency / leverage / search-space effect
→ contamination boundary
→ traceability/reuse assessment
→ disposition
```

Useful dispositions may be expressed in prose, for example:

- operationally sufficient now;
- useful resource exists but access/citation path is weak;
- source exists but not yet qualified for automated/local use;
- important knowledge-access gap;
- no meaningful gain over current source-internal method;
- external specialist review remains the appropriate escalation.

Do not introduce a new controlled vocabulary unless repeated execution proves it necessary.

## Relationship to #143

#143 remains the specific external lexical/genre-resource audit for FWB, DRW, HTR-United, DigiRef and reuse-rights/access questions.

This Issue must **not absorb or broaden #143 into a universal resource catalogue**. Instead:

- use #143 where lexical/genre candidate access is the real gap;
- identify additional bounded gaps in palaeography, diplomatics/Aktenkunde, editions, regional/institutional history, visitation practice, metrology/accounting notation, onomastics or other already-routed domains;
- open/extend only narrowly justified resource work after a real fixture demonstrates value.

## Relationship to #123 / evidence packet

- #123 remains the active scholarly owner and finite A–H research work package.
- This audit does not change the current #123 cursor or diplomatic truth.
- The post-#145 evidence packet remains epistemically clean and candidate-neutral by default.
- Knowledge resources enter only after the applicable source-only observation boundary and retain zero diplomatic evidence authority until target adjudication.
- No resource is auto-activated merely because it exists.

## External research rule

After the A–G audit identifies a concrete gap, external research should be narrow and problem-shaped, e.g.:

- qualified, citable references for early-modern central-German chancery/display capitals;
- searchable scholarly editions or corpora of sixteenth-century visitation records;
- specialist references for historical accounting/measure notation;
- regional onomastic or administrative references for a concrete identification problem.

Avoid generic searches such as “best palaeography tools” unless a real fixture cannot be expressed more specifically.

## Non-goals

- no new competency/domain map;
- no knowledge ontology or universal knowledge base;
- no bulk bibliography project;
- no automatic corpus ingestion;
- no candidate majority vote or confidence fusion;
- no weakening of source-only observation / target-return discipline;
- no external wording as manuscript evidence;
- no source-wide transcript, lexicon, hand registry or comparator corpus merely for future convenience;
- no new software/service before repeated value is demonstrated;
- no blocking of direct #123 source work when it can proceed safely without a knowledge-access dependency.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] material A–F4 expert moves have been sampled retrospectively from their actual decision/evidence history;
- [ ] G has been used prospectively to test at least one non-trivial expertise-activation path after source-only observation;
- [ ] each tested path distinguishes known/findable/accessed/inspected/operationalized rather than treating resource existence as competence;
- [ ] latency, diagnostic leverage, search-space reduction, traceability, contamination risk and reuse value have been assessed proportionally;
- [ ] a short prioritized list of real knowledge-access gaps exists;
- [ ] #143 remains a bounded lexical/genre/reuse-rights strand rather than being inflated into a universal owner;
- [ ] external research targets are specific enough to search directly;
- [ ] no new canonical knowledge infrastructure is introduced without demonstrated recurring benefit;
- [ ] useful resources that prove recurring value have a clear, smallest-next-step integration recommendation; non-useful paths are explicitly dropped.
