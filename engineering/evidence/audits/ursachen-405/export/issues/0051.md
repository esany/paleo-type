## Text

Implements the smallest source/provenance infrastructure justified by GOV-M2/GOV-M3 requirements and the current Knau corpus.

What changes:
- add `corpus/source-contract.json` as a small machine-readable contract for stable source/file/project/crosswalk invariants;
- add stdlib-only `tools/source_contract.py` with `validate` and deterministic `resolve` commands;
- run validation automatically on relevant PR/push changes;
- add a lean new-source intake/crosswalk template;
- remove the two source-less `META-CSV-*` rows from `corpus/files.tsv` (metadata batches are not historical-source instantiations; their research use remains documented in inspection/source-audit history);
- retain IMG132–135 explicitly as legacy provenance, never as current project primary files;
- align Reg. Ii 3 / `stat_visitation_00000197` with the confirmed record title `Visitation in Jena, Neustadt an der Orla, Pößneck, Saalfeld`;
- document resolver use in `corpus/README.md`.

The four source records remain distinct:
1. Reg. Ii 4 Bd. II — Visitation im Amt Ziegenrück, 294r–313r;
2. Reg. Ii 5 — Visitation im Amt Ziegenrück, 55v–66v, Registratur/Superintendenz Neustadt an der Orla (Auszug);
3. Reg. Ii 3 — Visitation in Jena, Neustadt an der Orla, Pößneck, Saalfeld;
4. Reg. Ii 3 — Pfarreinkommen Gössitz/Knau/Laskau/Bahren/Liebschütz/Liebengrün, 303r–306r.

Non-goals: no database, schema framework, importer, UI, TEI/RDF conversion, or transcription migration. Missing optional URNs are allowed; unresolved mappings remain unresolved. The validator only enforces deterministic structure/references.
