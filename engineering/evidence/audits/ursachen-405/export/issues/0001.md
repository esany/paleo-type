## Text

## Research benefit
Provide standards-based interchange/publication when the working corpus/transcription model is stable enough to justify it.

## Why not now
TSV/Markdown/YAML are currently faster to inspect, diff and correct. A TEI authoring workflow would add cost before it improves the active research bottleneck.

## Trigger
At least one coherent source unit is sufficiently transcribed/collated that a standards-based edition/export is an actual deliverable.

## Definition of Done
- map source/witness IDs, folios, hands, readings, variants, responsibility and facsimile references to the then-current TEI P5 without information loss;
- support manuscript description and critical-apparatus concepts required by the edition concept;
- validate generated TEI against an explicit schema/profile;
- keep working truth in lean canonical records unless a deliberate migration is approved.

## Trigger review
Re-check when a project reaches/approaches G6 or publication/export begins. If the trigger remains unmet, leave deferred; if superseded by another interchange solution, close with rationale.
