## Demonstrated problem

## Target workflow
For a **material** research-system change, use:

`analysis → requirements → concept → owner review/iteration → explicit implementation admission → development → tests/loss checks → result review`

Meaning:
1. **Analysis** — inspect current canonical state and demonstrated failure/need.
2. **Requirements** — derive applicable scholarly, epistemic, provenance, usability and technical requirements; identify affected disciplines.
3. **Concept** — propose the smallest coherent change, alternatives, dependencies and non-goals.
4. **Owner review/iteration** — the owner may correct project purpose, priorities or architecture choices.
5. **Implementation admission** — development begins only after explicit owner authorization of a sufficiently precise bounded scope. An already precise and explicitly admitted plan/Issue may serve as that authorization; routine implementation details do not require repeated micro-confirmation.
6. **Development** — stay inside the admitted scope.
7. **Tests/loss checks** — deterministic checks plus scholarly/provenance/regression checks proportional to the change.
8. **Review** — compare the result against the admitted requirements and preserved evidence; CI success alone is insufficient.

## Consequence classes
The later canonical workflow should distinguish at least:
- routine source/research work;
- derived/rebuildable output;
- canonical research/data migration;
- methodology/architecture change;
- governing-objective change.

The consequence determines the review boundary. Do not turn every source observation, transcription correction or evidence log into an approval ceremony.

## Owner vs specialist boundary
Owner approval governs purpose, priorities and material architecture/change admission. It does **not** turn the owner into a substitute palaeographer, archivist, historian or other specialist reviewer.

Consequential specialist judgements remain unresolved or are escalated to qualified independent review when required by the method/evidence. Owner approval and specialist validation are distinct statuses.
