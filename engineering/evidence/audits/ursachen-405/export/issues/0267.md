# Issue #267: [BUG] Large project-root source survey lacks canonical granular inspection coverage

## Text

## Status / authority

**BUG / ANALYSIS RECORD ONLY. No new inspection-state schema or workflow is authorized by this Issue.**

## Demonstrated trigger

The Wolkenburg Project fixture differs operationally from the currently modeled small bounded source-group uses:

- individual archival units are expected to be roughly 50–300 pages long;
- the Human Owner cannot identify the relevant sections/chapters in advance;
- therefore the selected archival unit must initially be **surveyed across its full extent** to discover relevant internal material;
- later research may narrow to particular sections/loci, but the discovery survey cannot safely begin from a preselected excerpt.

The existing repository does not expose a canonical, granular owner for “which ranges/pages/folios of this Project root have actually been inspected for relevance and at what depth.”

## Current support and gap

Current surfaces are insufficient for this specific state:

- `corpus/sources.tsv: inspection_status` is a coarse Source-level status, not range-level coverage;
- `project.yml: active_scope` states current scholarly scope, not what portions have already been inspected;
- `STATUS.md` may summarize progress, but prose alone is not a stable granular coverage owner;
- source crosswalks map identifiers and must not be repurposed as inspection-progress tables;
- #64 used a folio-by-folio progress matrix, but explicitly treated it as a work/review aid rather than canonical research truth;
- #12 governs catalogue/repository **discovery coverage**, not inspection coverage *inside one already selected Source*.

## Failure modes

Without explicit source-internal survey coverage, a future context can easily:

1. mistake “some sections inspected” for “whole archival unit examined”;
2. rescan already inspected ranges because no durable cursor exists;
3. skip ranges because relevance was inferred from neighbouring headings or metadata;
4. make negative claims (“nothing else relevant”) without full-source support;
5. lose the distinction between quick relevance survey, detailed source description, diplomatic transcription and later analysis;
6. encode page-by-page progress ad hoc in STATUS prose or chat;
7. narrow `active_scope` after discovery and thereby erase the fact/basis that the remaining root had already been surveyed.

## Scholarly distinction that must survive

At minimum, the model/workflow must keep separate:

- **selected Project root** — whole archival unit;
- **current active research scope** — may be whole root during discovery, later narrower;
- **inspection/survey coverage** — which loci were actually looked at;
- **inspection depth/purpose** — e.g. relevance survey versus transcription-level inspection;
- **resulting discovered internal Sources/sections** — created only when evidence/identity/research requires them;
- **negative/completeness claim basis** — only as strong as actual coverage.

“Surveyed all pages for relevance” must not be silently equated with “fully transcribed” or “all textual phenomena resolved.”

## Requirements implicated

- REQ-ARCH-005 — inspection must remain distinct from catalogue metadata/inference;
- REQ-ARCH-008 — absence/completeness claims require documented boundaries and access/inspection basis;
- REQ-GWP-002 — consequential evidence remains recoverable to exact Source/locus;
- REQ-GWP-008 — restartability without old chat;
- REQ-UX-007 — current state/gaps/next action visible to a human;
- research-gate rule that gate claims are scope/locus-specific rather than automatically source-wide.

## Relation to existing Issues

- **#12 OPEN:** complementary, not duplicate. #12 is corpus discovery/search coverage across repositories/catalogues; this Issue is full/partial inspection coverage inside one already selected archival Source/Project root.
- **#64 historical source-research fixture:** demonstrated explicit folio-level progress and the need to distinguish `not observed` / `not investigated`; its progress matrix was intentionally non-canonical.
- **#69 CLOSED:** its owner matrix referred generically to “project-specific inspection state” but did not establish a stable representation/owner for large-source survey coverage.
- **#265:** `active_scope` null/whole-root semantics must not be used as a substitute for inspection coverage.
- **#244 OPEN:** separate engineering-information bugwave; this scholarly inspection-state gap does not change its readiness/admission.

## Analysis required

Use at least one 50–300 page Wolkenburg archival unit as the real fixture and compare:

1. STATUS-only prose;
2. an existing project research/audit structure, if one can truthfully own granular coverage;
3. the smallest additional structured coverage record if current owners are insufficient.

Determine the minimum unit required in practice (folio, page, range, chapter/section, export image, or another evidenced locus) rather than hard-coding page-level granularity in advance.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] a fresh context can determine exactly which portions of a large Project root have been surveyed/inspected and which have not;
- [ ] survey coverage is distinct from `active_scope`, gate attainment and transcription completeness;
- [ ] inspection depth/purpose is not silently flattened into one boolean `inspected` state where that would mislead;

## Analysis result — reuse the existing inspection-log concept, but make its ownership/coverage semantics explicit

Fresh repository inspection found an important existing precedent that narrows this Issue substantially: `projects/ega-reg-ii-5-knau/sources/inspection-log.tsv` already records project-local inspection events with `corpus_source_id`, `unit_inspected`, access basis, what the inspection supports and limitations. It includes metadata-only rows, file-level inspection and exact folio/image/PDF-page inspection, including the #64 Checkpoint-A span `61v–63r`.

So the demonstrated gap is **not** “paleo-type has no way at all to record inspection.” The gap is that this useful pattern is Project-specific/legacy, not part of the generic Project contract/template, and it does not yet provide stable generic semantics for progressive whole-root survey coverage.

### Stable distinction

Keep separate:

- Project root — whole independent Archivale;
- current scope state — #265 (`none | whole-root | bounded` conceptually);
- inspection coverage — what source ranges/units were actually inspected;
- inspection purpose/depth — what kind of inspection occurred;
- discovered internal Sources — introduced only when stable identity/provenance/citation/research requires them;
- gate attainment/transcription completeness — separate scholarly maturity claims.

### Minimum information requirement for a reusable coverage owner

Without fixing column names or path yet, each consequential coverage record needs enough information to reconstruct:

1. the canonical Source being inspected;
2. the exact inspected coverage/locus in an evidenced locator domain — folio/page/export image/range as actually available, never an untyped bare number;
3. inspection purpose/depth (for example relevance/structural survey versus detailed source inspection versus diplomatic work), without prematurely freezing a universal ontology;
4. exact access/evidence basis where consequential, including File/digital locator when available;
5. result/support plus explicit limitations/unresolved material;
6. date/responsibility/provenance to the degree needed for restartability.

The representation should support ranges, not force one row per page/image. Page/image-level rows are valid when that is the only truthful locator.

### What “whole Archivale examined” minimally means for the Wolkenburg discovery phase

Given the owner requirement that relevant passages cannot be preselected, a defensible **whole-root relevance survey** requires every available page/image to be visually examined in source order at least deeply enough to:

- determine whether it contains potentially relevant material;
- notice/document significant section/document boundaries or headings needed to avoid skipping material;
- mark unreadable/missing/unavailable units as unresolved rather than silently covered;
- preserve discovered candidate loci for later detailed work.

It does **not** require diplomatic transcription of every page. Detailed transcription/palaeographic analysis is triggered only where the later scholarly scope requires it. Therefore:

`whole-root survey complete ≠ G2 complete ≠ fully transcribed`

### Smallest ownership direction

The existing Reg. Ii inspection log is the strongest Generic-Fit starting point. The likely smallest solution is to standardize an **optional Project-local canonical inspection-coverage log** rather than invent a corpus-wide work tracker. `STATUS.md` should summarize/point to it; crosswalks continue to own identifier correspondence, not work progress.

The exact path (`sources/inspection-log.tsv`, `research/...`, etc.) should be chosen only after loss-checking the existing Reg. Ii log and the Wolkenburg fixture. No duplicate progress owner should be created.

### Acceptance consequence

A whole-root coverage claim is valid only when the recorded inspected ranges cover the declared available root extent in the applicable locator/crosswalk, with unresolved/missing material explicit. Narrowing later `active_scope` must not erase that completed coverage evidence.

### Admission classification

Standardizing this Project-local canonical owner and its contract/context semantics is a **material shared Project/research-state change**. Analyse/requirements/concept are now sufficiently bounded; implementation should be admitted together with #265 and tested against both Reg. Ii 5 and a real Wolkenburg 61/135-image fixture.

**Disposition:** concept ready for combined D-016 decision with #265; do not create a separate page-tracker subsystem.

## Kommentar 5985666297 · 2026-10-04T23:38:57Z · esany

## Coverage refinement — inspection can span the root without making the root research scope

#270/#265 now clarify an important separation that this Issue must preserve.

The earlier phrase **“whole-root relevance survey”** remains meaningful only as a possible **inspection/discovery coverage target**: when relevance cannot be bounded in advance, the researcher may need to examine every available page/image in source order deeply enough to avoid silently missing relevant documents or boundaries. It must **not** be interpreted as saying the whole root is the active scholarly scope.

### Coverage semantics to preserve

Inspection coverage must be able to say, independently of research relevance:

- what Source/locus/page/image range was actually examined;
- at what inspection purpose/depth;
- on what exact evidence/File/locator basis where available;
- what was found/candidate-relevant;
- what was inspected and excluded as not relevant to the current research question;
- what remains partial, unreadable, missing, unavailable or unresolved;
- enough ordering/boundary information to demonstrate that material was not silently skipped when a complete-root survey claim is made.

A document can therefore be **fully inspected for relevance and not enter active research scope**. Conversely, a relevant internal Source/locus can enter research scope before the rest of the root has completed inspection coverage.

The recursive documentary structure from #270 also means coverage must not assume one flat page-range model. A long Archivale may contain document complexes, documents, sections, registers, accounts, attachments or other Source/locus levels. Coverage claims need exact locators appropriate to the evidence actually available without forcing every discovered unit to become a canonical Source.

The prior distinction still holds:

`inspection complete for relevance ≠ G2 complete ≠ fully transcribed`.

The existing Reg. Ii 5 inspection-log pattern remains a useful Generic-Fit precursor, but any shared canonical representation/contract remains material under D-016 and must be loss-checked rather than introduced by this analysis comment alone.
