## Problem / Gap
Prior lifecycle work mixed `DEV-READY`, decision-ready and implementation-ready language, while a real Human interaction proved that formal acknowledgement can occur without understanding the stage/consequence. Human relay elimination must not turn the Owner into a semantic confirmation proxy.

## Findings covered
F12, F20, F21, F22, F23, F34 plus new findings F37 (readiness must name the next admissible activity), F38 (Human-decision-readiness jargon itself can obscure the decision), and F39 (anti-regression detail loops must not become a standing blocker when bounded work is ready).

## Required effect
Define a proportional work/readiness contract in plain language:
- ready for analysis/inquiry/design/implementation/validation/Human decision are distinct claims;
- dependencies and acceptance are visible on the bounded Issue;
- Human intervention is required only for irreducible authority/judgement;
- before a material Human decision, the system explains problem, evidence, current/proposed state, alternatives, effects, residual risk, exact authority requested and non-effects without requiring audit replay;
- this is a usability/situational-awareness test, not a quiz or extra approval ceremony.

## Next activity / readiness
**READY TO DESIGN AND VALIDATE THE DECISION/READINESS CONTRACT. NOT READY FOR D-016 IMPLEMENTATION ADMISSION.**

## Dependencies
#245 coverage, #248 ADR inventory, and enough #246/#247 design to describe what a future decision would actually authorize.

## Acceptance / close condition
- readiness claims always identify the next activity;
- bounded Issues expose dependencies/DoR/DoD proportionally without heavyweight empty templates;
- an owner-facing brief can be understood without the audit genealogy;
- Human is not asked to reconstruct, route, plan technical sequencing or repeat known context;
- routine already-authorized work can proceed without `weiter`/`ausführen` confirmation;
- material ambiguity still returns to Human authority rather than being inferred.

## Non-goals
No formal universal state machine, workflow engine, automatic authority inference or Human comprehension score.

## Whole-system return
State what is now ready for which activity, what remains design/evidence blocked, and whether a real D-016 decision surface can finally be presented.
