> **Pre-priority research-direction formation.** When a material decision about the next research direction is actually needed and no Human-Owner-selected scope or question exists, do not infer priority from unresolved, available or easily addressable work. This requirement does not prescribe a fixed number of candidates, scoring/ranking, a persistent Candidate-Question object, a fixed competence topology, a preferred AI role or a separate Research-Framing layer.

This wording is solution-neutral and keeps the trigger narrow: it applies only when a material direction decision is actually needed and evidence supports plurality; it does not require every terminal research state to generate new work.
