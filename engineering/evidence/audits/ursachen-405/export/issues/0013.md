## Text

## Research benefit
Structured bibliography becomes useful when repeated citation metadata creates maintenance drift or publication requires exchange.

## Why not now
The inspected literature set is small enough that added bibliography infrastructure would not improve active source work.

## Trigger
Repeated citation maintenance becomes a demonstrated burden or publication/export requires structured bibliography exchange.

## Definition of Done
- adopt a standard exchange format such as BibTeX or CSL JSON without creating a second source-status store;
- retain exact inspected-passage/access status in the research audit;
- bibliographic metadata never implies full-text inspection;
- avoid duplicating canonical research-source status.

## Trigger review
Re-check when citation duplication becomes recurrent or publication/export begins. Otherwise leave deferred.
