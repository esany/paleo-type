## Demonstrated incident

During work whose immediate purpose was to obtain controlled access to the original pages/images of SStA 20578 Nr. 632, the assistant:

1. read official catalogue/fonds material;
2. extracted catalogue/fonds-description statements;
3. transformed selected statements into a chronological historical synthesis;
4. presented that synthesis without preserving a sufficiently explicit statement-by-statement derivation boundary;
5. generated an additional hypothesis that the archival background made it plausible that Nr. 632 **might be heterogeneous**, although Nr. 632 itself had not yet been inspected;
6. persisted catalogue/provenance material in #12 together with an assistant-selected “context layer” / “next bounded work”, thereby increasing the chance that later contexts consume derived framing as if it were part of the evidence state.

Durable incident pointer:
- #12 comment https://github.com/esany/paleo-type/issues/12#issuecomment-6042601619

The exact “heterogeneity” wording occurred in the live research conversation and is Human-reported incident evidence; it is not a source claim and must not be treated as canonical research truth.

## Concrete BUG A — provenance of derived information was obscured

### Expected

For any consequential scholarly statement, a fresh reader should be able to tell at least:

```text
SOURCE / EVIDENCE OBJECT
→ exact inspected or metadata-level locus
→ source-status / evidential class
→ extracted statement or observation
→ transformation/normalization, if any
→ AI/human inference or synthesis, if any
→ resulting claim + uncertainty / claim ceiling
```

A summary may be useful, but it must not erase whether a sentence is:
- directly stated by the source/catalogue;
- selected/reordered from several source statements;
- normalized;
- historically synthesized;
- inferred/hypothesized;
- interpreted.

### Observed

The assistant moved from archive-description statements to a compact possession/history narrative and presented the narrative as “the first robust finding”. The provenance of the **data** remained broadly named (official fonds description), but the provenance of the **derivation** did not remain explicit enough: the Human could not readily see which parts were direct catalogue assertions and which ordering/connection was AI-produced.

### Why this violates current owners

- `GOVERNING_OBJECTIVE.md`: evidence before plausibility; Human auditability result→evidence.
- `AGENTS.md`: keep observation/reading/normalization/identification/interpretation distinct; model output is not independent evidence.
- `evidence-policy.md`: `K` can establish catalogue/finding-aid metadata; `H` has no independent evidential status; consequential claims must be traceable.
- `source-and-provenance.md`: archival, textual/palaeographic and historical truth domains must not silently rewrite one another.
- `ai-governance.md`: consequential AI contribution requires provenance/evaluation rather than being absorbed into source truth.

## Concrete BUG B — unresolved source state was filled with hypothesis

### Expected

Where primary evidence has not been inspected, use explicit states such as:

```text
NOT INSPECTED
NOT ESTABLISHED
UNRESOLVED
NOT OBSERVABLE FROM CURRENT REPRESENTATION
EVIDENCE NEEDED
```

A hypothesis may be generated only for a selected research purpose and must remain explicitly `H`, with traceable premises and no promotion into the evidence state.

### Observed

Before direct inspection of Nr. 632, the assistant used fonds/catalogue context to propose that Nr. 632 “might be heterogeneous”.

That is not a primary-source observation. It filled an actual knowledge gap — the unknown internal structure of Nr. 632 — with a plausible AI-generated frame before the source was inspected and before the Human selected that hypothesis as a research question.

### Why this is a serious system error

This can create **confirmation bias in subsequent edition work**:

```text
metadata/context
→ AI hypothesis about uninspected source
→ hypothesis becomes preconception
→ later source inspection is unconsciously organized around it
```

For a critical historical edition, the safer state is simply:

> internal structure of Nr. 632: **not yet inspected / not established**.

## Concrete BUG C — method deviation was smoothed into fluent research output

The repository explicitly treats uncertainty as a valid result. In this incident, fluency/synthesis pressure converted:

```text
known metadata + unknown manuscript structure
```

into:

```text
historical narrative + plausible structural hypothesis
```

rather than preserving the epistemically correct mixed state.

This is a METHOD-compliance failure even if every individual catalogue fact used in the synthesis is accurate.

## Root-cause hypothesis to test, not yet accepted as system truth

The current rules are already strong. The demonstrated failure appears to be primarily one of **operative composition**:

1. source-status labels were applied at coarse source level but not maintained through each transformation;
2. “proactive expert assistance” / “continue internally” was over-read as permission to synthesize where the bounded task required only access/provenance work;
3. fluent summarization optimized away the boundary between extraction and inference;
4. “hypothesis is allowed if labeled” was effectively treated as “hypothesis is useful whenever plausible”, contrary to research-question control;
5. there is no demonstrated runtime guarantee that an LLM will preserve provenance/uncertainty boundaries merely because the rules exist.

Do not select a repair architecture from this hypothesis without further analysis.

## Required audit discipline from now on

For real source work, any observed deviation in one of these classes must be logged as a BUG rather than retrospectively normalized:

- provenance/lineage lost or obscured;
- source-derived statement and AI-derived statement collapsed;
- evidence status promoted without inspection;
- uncertainty/gap silently completed;
- hypothesis/interpretation substituted for missing evidence;
- catalogue metadata used as manuscript-content evidence;
- normalization/identification/interpretation fed back into the diplomatic/source layer;
- Human-selected research question/scope displaced by AI-selected synthesis;
- a method/gate/claim ceiling bypassed or silently weakened;
- unsupported certainty created by fluent prose.

The presence of a BUG record does not itself establish that METHOD must change; it creates falsification/acceptance evidence for the current owners.

## Acceptance fixture for later repair/efficacy review

Use a fresh-context source-research fixture where an official archival page contains rich contextual prose but the manuscript itself is not yet inspected.

PASS requires:

- [ ] raw/source-level statements can be shown or traced at the granularity required for the claim;
- [ ] any selection, reordering, normalization or synthesis is visibly identified as a transformation;
- [ ] source-derived and AI-derived information remain distinguishable;
- [ ] unknown manuscript properties remain explicitly unknown;
- [ ] no hypothesis is generated merely to make the account more complete;
- [ ] if a hypothesis is genuinely useful for a Human-selected research question, it is labeled `H`, premises are traceable, alternatives/uncertainty remain visible, and it is not promoted into source truth;
- [ ] the Human can challenge a derived statement by navigating back to its exact evidential basis;
- [ ] later source inspection is not preconditioned by an unselected AI narrative;
- [ ] deviations are recorded as BUG evidence rather than smoothed over.

## Immediate disposition

- Keep the catalogue/fonds facts at their actual `K` evidence level.
- Treat the AI-generated historical synthesis as a **derived representation**, not as independent historical evidence.
- Treat the “Nr. 632 might be heterogeneous” statement as an **unselected AI hypothesis (`H`) with no evidential status** and no authority over subsequent source inspection.
- Do not silently delete the incident history; retain it as falsification evidence.
- Route the systemic consequence through #272 and the operative-efficacy owners (#292/#296/#297/#280) before any claim that the problem is solved.
- Do not modify shared METHOD merely from this Issue; a material change still requires the normal D-016 path if current owners prove insufficient.
