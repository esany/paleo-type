## Research question

Across Nr. 632, which candidate writings can be recognized at useful overview depth as, for example:

- petition / supplication / administrative letter;
- formal charter / Lehnbrief-like instrument;
- Lehn(s)revers / counter-instrument;
- Lehnschein;
- Vollmacht;
- concept/draft;
- copy/transcript / certified copy;
- chancery/registry/authentication note;
- endorsement / exterior address / dorsal note;
- enclosure or supporting record;
- other / unresolved;

and what date, actors, and business function can be recovered with short diagnostic readings?

The list is an open comparison vocabulary, **not** a closed ontology.

## Method

Work from the whole-domain research map and direct visual inspection, but do **not** repeat a blind 135-image survey.

For each meaningful content unit/candidate, record only what changes understanding:

- provider image/PDF page span;
- material/view relation if relevant;
- candidate writing/document function;
- short diagnostic opening/heading/closing reading where needed;
- date or date range if legible;
- named persons/offices only where confidently readable;
- apparent role in the Lehnsverfahren/business file;
- confidence and alternatives;
- exact unresolved question.

Keep `OBSERVATION → READING → IDENTIFICATION → INTERPRETATION` distinct.

## Human-review ergonomics

When a Human visual check is actually needed:

- provide only the exact extracted PDF page(s) or small crops needed for the question;
- state the mapping explicitly, e.g. `PDF p. 61 = provider image 0061`;
- ask one concrete visual question at a time;
- do not send the Human back to the 135-page PDF for navigation;
- do not ask the Human to perform palaeographic/specialist adjudication merely because AI confidence is low.

Create a Project research inventory/table that gives the Human Owner a comprehensible answer to **'Welche Quellen/Schriftstücke sind in Nr. 632 enthalten?'**

`range | candidate writing/function | date | actors | role in business process | direct evidence | confidence | unresolved`

Approximate ranges are acceptable where exact physical/documentary boundaries remain unresolved.
