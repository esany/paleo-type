## Work

For each defensible transaction/event cluster, reconstruct only as far as evidence allows:

- date / sequence;
- petitioner, vassal, lord/chancery or other actors;
- initiating writing/request;
- supporting evidence or older copied material;
- chancery processing / presentation / authentication;
- resulting certificate, formal instrument, Revers or related record;
- later annotations or reuse;
- unresolved competing sequences.

Use short diagnostic readings rather than full transcription.

Do not force the whole archive into one linear chronology where several overlapping or repeated Lehnsfälle are represented.

## Important constraints

- archival order ≠ business order;
- represented date ≠ date of the photographed copy carrier;
- copy label ≠ demonstrated exact template relation;
- similar formula ≠ same transaction;
- same person/date ≠ sufficient proof of documentary relation.

## Deliverable

A chronological / process map of the major Lehnsfälle or business clusters represented in Nr. 632, with explicit evidence and unresolved relations.

Suggested form:

`cluster | approximate date(s) | participating writings | actors | business-process role | evidence | unresolved relation`
