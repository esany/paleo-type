## Text

## Research benefit
A corpus-wide hand registry becomes useful only if demonstrably recurring hands materially improve comparison or prevent duplicated identification work.

## Why not now
The corpus does not yet establish recurring hands strongly enough to justify a separate registry; project palaeographic records are sufficient.

## Trigger
At least two source units show a plausible recurring hand **and** that identification materially affects comparison, transcription or validation.

## Definition of Done
- create stable local hand IDs only where evidence warrants them;
- keep hand identification separate from scribe/person identification;
- record comparison anchors and uncertainty;
- never infer same hand from date, office or textual similarity alone;
- avoid duplicating project palaeographic evidence.

## Trigger review
Re-check when cross-source hand recurrence is actually observed. Otherwise leave deferred.
