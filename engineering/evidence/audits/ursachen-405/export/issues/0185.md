## Motivation from existing analysis

The current problem landscape explicitly leaves C4 / cross-source genericity unresolved. Reg. Ii 5 is a rich and unusually well-documented fixture, but it must not silently define a generic research system.

The purpose of this workstream is to determine which observed mechanisms and scholarly needs recur when source structure, research purpose, evidence situation and competency combination materially differ.

## Comparative dimensions

Do not vary `Source type` alone. For every comparison carry at least:

```text
Source type
Project / research purpose
current knowledge state
evidence situation
research task / question
materially implicated competencies
```

This prevents a Project-purpose or evidence-state difference from being misreported as a source-type effect.

## Fixture strategy

Use Reg. Ii 5 as one comparison point, not as the template.

Add at least two materially different source/project structures where feasible, preferably including:

- an individual letter / narrative / person-centred source;
- a serial accounting / administrative source.

Existing repository Projects may be used if they provide sufficient direct evidence and genuinely different research structure. If the repository does not contain an adequate fixture, use a narrowly bounded, publicly inspectable external historical example and document its provenance and limitations.

A fixture is not included merely to satisfy a count. `insufficient comparable fixture` is preferable to a weak analogy.

## Research questions

Investigate without presupposing a generic capability model:

- which competencies are materially implicated and which are not;
- how the research question emerges or changes;
- when context is contaminating, neutral or productively necessary;
- whether disciplinary interaction changes what evidence is sought;
- where independent judgement is important and where isolation would impoverish the problem;
- which object/unit distinctions recur and which disappear;
- which Requirement-efficacy problems recur;
- whether previously observed mechanisms survive the change in source/project structure;
- what appears genuinely source-/purpose-specific.

## External disciplinary literature

Use authoritative or high-quality relevant literature to challenge and contextualize fixture findings, not to pre-fill them.

Preferred sequence:

```text
direct fixture analysis
→ provisional interpretation and uncertainty
→ literature for challenge / comparison / expansion
```

For material external claims record:

- source/reference;
- claim supported;
- epistemic role (`empirical`, `methodological`, `normative`, `technical standard`, `analogical`);
- relevance to the concrete comparison;
- conflicting or limiting evidence where found.

Do not turn framework vocabulary into the fixture's problem ontology merely because it is available.

## Generality discipline

Keep separate:

- single-fixture observation;
- within-project recurrence;
- cross-competence convergence on one fixture;
- cross-fixture recurrence;
- cross-source recurrence;
- provisional generic hypothesis;
- source-specific / contradicted;
- insufficient evidence.

Do not claim `strong generic evidence` from a small deliberately heterogeneous sample.

## HCI / owner evidence boundary

Where owner-facing decision needs are discussed, distinguish at least:

- literature-supported;
- repository-history-supported;
- scenario-tested;
- human-observed.

Do not claim that a concrete decision surface works empirically for the Human Owner without corresponding evidence.

## Completion criteria

- [ ] Reg. Ii 5 is treated as a fixture rather than a universal template;
- [ ] at least two materially different comparison structures are investigated, or inadequacy of available fixtures is explicitly documented;
- [ ] source type is separated from research purpose/evidence state/task;
- [ ] competency participation is problem-driven;
- [ ] primary fixture interpretation precedes literature-based reframing where practicable;
- [ ] cross-source recurrence is distinguished from cross-competence agreement;
- [ ] counterexamples and source-specific findings are preserved;
- [ ] generality claims remain provisional and proportional to evidence;
- [ ] findings are persisted for later integration under #183.

## Dependency / downstream

Can run in parallel with #184.

Its frozen findings are required input for the later integration/Concept-Challenge Issue so that no generic synthesis is based on Reg. Ii 5 alone.
