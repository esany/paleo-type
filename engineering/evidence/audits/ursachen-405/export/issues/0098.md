## Depends on

- #97 complete or sufficiently advanced to establish stable archival/line addresses and no omitted visible units.

## Method
For each consequential unresolved/working locus:
1. localize the exact target grapheme/sign sequence at stable folio/line address;
2. preserve the prior first-reading state and any local comparison already used in #96;
3. classify the problem: grapheme, abbreviation, numeral/accounting sign, segmentation, historical word form, possible source phenomenon, hand issue, etc.;
4. inventory relevant Reg. Ii 4 internal anchors across the full locus;
5. determine whether the anchors are palaeographically comparable; same record/source is not automatically same hand;
6. compare discriminating graphical features rather than word silhouette or semantic plausibility;
7. treat abbreviations, numerals and monetary/metrological/accounting notation as potentially distinct sign systems;
8. use philological/lexical/domain context only after graphical candidate formation;
9. record graphically viable alternatives, positive and negative evidence, and reasons for promotion/rejection;
10. retain `unresolved` where the evidence does not decide;
11. ensure consequential re-adjudications remain traceable rather than silently overwriting the first-reading history.
