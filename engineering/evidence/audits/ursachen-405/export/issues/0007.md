## Resolution
Absorbed into the generic scholarly method and G6 rather than kept as a second publication-quality checklist.

`methodology/research-gates.md` G6 now requires an explicit edition concept, declared conventions, source-to-output audit, visible uncertainty/interventions, evidence-based commentary, traceability and proportionate independent specialist review. `methodology/historical-source-workflow.md` S9 defines the publication/edition activity and its relationship to G6.

Rights/licensing and dissemination-specific checks remain requirements to apply when an actual publication target exists; they do not justify a permanently open duplicate governance issue.

Closed to preserve one canonical methodological home and avoid a dead/parallel checklist.
