## Problem claim

The repository already contains strong scholarly semantics across METHOD, workflow, evidence policy and domain Requirements. A Human can nevertheless be shown opaque labels such as:

- `S4`;
- `reconnaissance`;
- `G2 active`;
- an Issue number;
- a generic `CURRENT CURSOR`;

without seeing what is actually being tested, which competence governs the question, what evidence can decide it, what conclusion is currently permitted, what remains unproved and why the proposed next action is discriminating.

For a non-specialist Human Owner this leaves the scholarly process too close to a black box even when the underlying method is correct.

## Desired outcome

For consequential source work, expose a Human-readable **scholarly action claim** with these required meanings:

```text
CURRENT OBSERVATION / UNCERTAINTY
→ BOUNDED SCHOLARLY QUESTION
→ GOVERNING DISCIPLINE / COMPETENCE
→ EVIDENCE OR TEST NEEDED
→ CLAIM CEILING / WHAT IS NOT ESTABLISHED
→ NEXT DISCRIMINATING ACTION
→ SPECIALIST ESCALATION TRIGGER, if applicable
```

This is a derived/execution projection of canonical scholarly state and METHOD. It is **not** a new canonical claim graph or a replacement for Project research truth.

## Competence-routing requirement

The Human should not have to know in advance that a problem belongs to palaeography, diplomatics, historical philology, metrology, archival science or another auxiliary discipline.

The assistant should route from the observed source question:

```text
source phenomenon / question
→ governing discipline
→ appropriate method
→ required evidence
→ admissible conclusion
→ escalation threshold
```

Examples to test:

- hard grapheme → palaeography / internal graphical comparison;
- document function or transmission stage → diplomatics / documentary evidence;
- abbreviation or historical term → palaeography + philology, with written form preserved before expansion/semantics;
- accounting/measure notation → palaeography + metrology/accounting history;
- copy/extract relation → diplomatics/textual criticism + independent witness inspection/collation;
- person role such as scribe/issuer/recipient/owner → contextual archival/diplomatic/historical evidence rather than mention alone.

## Human-trust requirement

A current action should make it understandable:

- what was actually observed;
- what is only provisional/inferred;
- what evidence class is available;
- which claim is being tested;
- what stronger claim is currently forbidden;
- why the next action can discriminate among alternatives;
- when `unresolved` is the correct result;
- whether independent qualified specialist validation has or has not occurred.

Example of insufficient output:

> `S4 / reconnaissance continues.`

Example of the required semantic quality:

> I am comparing these inspected pages for documentary starts/ends, writing-characteristic changes and recurring letter/abbreviation forms because we do not yet know whether this candidate section is structurally distinct or sufficiently relevant for deeper work. This is archival/diplomatic plus palaeographic analysis. A heading or script change can support a hypothesis but does not establish a new Source or named hand. The next test is comparison of neighbouring boundaries and exact same-witness graphical anchors. If the evidence does not discriminate, the boundary/hand question remains provisional or unresolved.

The exact wording is not normative; the reconstructable meanings are.

### 1. Human need clarified by real use

The normal operating context is **scholarly use and research assistance**, not system-development work. Technology is support infrastructure. The relevant interface is therefore:

```text
scholarly task / knowledge need
→ determine what evidence/competence is needed
→ assess available technical means proportionally
→ act, explain uncertainty/limits, or involve the Human where useful/necessary
→ return to the scholarly task/outcome
```

The assistant must neither hide behind tooling nor pretend that every technical limitation can be solved autonomously.

A Human request is **not inherently a failure**. It is legitimate when the requested contribution is genuinely useful/non-derivable, proportionate to the task, and explained in terms of what evidence or authority it supplies. The failure is reflexive transfer of internal tool-routing burden to the Human without first assessing the task, capability situation and remaining uncertainty.

### 2. Real negative fixture

### 3. Required Human-facing semantic contract

Reuse the existing #292 action-claim semantics; refine them without creating a second methodology:

```text
CURRENT OBSERVATION / ESTABLISHED STATE
→ CURRENT UNCERTAINTY / NOT VERIFIED
→ BOUNDED SCHOLARLY QUESTION
→ GOVERNING COMPETENCE
→ EVIDENCE / TEST NEEDED
→ TASK / CAPABILITY ASSESSMENT
→ OPTIONS + MATERIAL TRADE-OFFS
→ CHOSEN NEXT DISCRIMINATING ACTION
→ CLAIM CEILING / WHAT IS NOT ESTABLISHED
→ HUMAN OR SPECIALIST INPUT, only where useful/necessary
```

These are required **meanings**, not a mandatory form, status enum or verbose checklist for every turn.

### 4. Uncertainty semantics

The assistance layer must preserve uncertainty instead of converting it into either confidence or blanket blockage. Where material, a Human should be able to distinguish semantically:

- what is directly established;
- what is provisional/inferred;
- what has not yet been checked;
- what is unknown because a concrete technical/access route failed;
- what remains unknown after proportionate available routes were tried;
- what requires new evidence;
- what requires Human authority/facts unavailable to the system;
- what requires qualified specialist judgement.

Do **not** preselect a universal machine enum for these distinctions. #291/evidence-policy/workflow remain the semantic owners of the underlying scholarly/evidence state; #292 owns the Human-facing assistance meaning.

### 5. Task/capability assessment requirement

Before a technical problem is translated into Human work, the assistant should make a proportionate professional judgement:

1. **What exactly is the scholarly information/action needed now?**
2. **How technically ordinary or difficult should obtaining it be in the present context?**
3. **Which available route(s) are relevant and proportionate to try?**
4. **What has actually been tried?**
5. **What does the observed failure establish, and what does it not establish?**
6. **What viable continuations remain?**
7. **Which continuation best advances the scholarly task with least unnecessary burden/risk?**

This is not a requirement to exhaust every imaginable tool or retry indefinitely. Bounded alternatives require expected information gain and must respect workflow no-progress/loop protection.

### 6. Human collaboration boundary

Allowed outcomes include all of the following:

- **assistant continues internally** when a reasonable derivable read-only/technical route remains;
- **assistant reports a real limit** when the available environment cannot perform the needed action reliably;
- **assistant asks the Human for a bounded contribution** when that is genuinely the best/only continuation;
- **assistant returns an authority question** when the missing fact is owner-reserved;
- **assistant preserves unresolved / escalates to a specialist** when the remaining question is specialist judgement rather than a Human-owner task.

When asking the Human for work, the request should state enough to understand:

```text
WHY this contribution is useful/necessary
WHAT exact evidence/fact/action is requested
WHAT the assistant has already established or failed to establish
WHAT claim/action becomes possible if supplied
WHAT remains uncertain even afterwards
```

Do not make the Human choose internal Chat/Work/CLI/provider/connector/runtime vocabulary merely because the implementation has those boundaries.

### 7. Required system properties for Dev

A later implementation/concept must preserve at least:

- **A1 — scholarly primacy:** the active research question/outcome remains the control objective; technical activity is support;
- **A2 — transparent uncertainty:** material unknown/provisional/not-verified state and its evidential reason remain visible;
- **A3 — claim ceiling:** technical success/failure, catalogue metadata, AI inference and source observation cannot be laundered across evidence levels;
