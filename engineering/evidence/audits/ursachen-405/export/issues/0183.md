## Motivation

The 2026-09-14 audit chain has reached a point where internal conceptual analysis is ahead of empirical validation:

- `requirements/research-framing-transdisciplinary-competency-audit-2026-09-14.md` identifies a less-visible pre-task transdisciplinary framing/research-selection function after lean-down;
- `requirements/research-system-problem-landscape-2026-09-14.md` shows that many construction sites are not obviously missing-Requirement problems, but may instead involve activation, sequencing, mechanism, visibility, freshness/propagation, function loss or enforcement;
- the same landscape leaves A1/A5/B1/B2/B4/B5 and cross-source genericity materially under-evidenced;
- `requirements/executable-transdisciplinary-research-system-concept-idea-2026-09-14.md` is already a rich solution hypothesis and therefore must not become the frame through which the missing evidence is manufactured.

Two symmetric research-design risks must remain open rather than solved by assumption:

```text
Concept Bias:
pre-existing conceptual frame / solution language
→ premise inheritance / confirmation bias / authority laundering

Decomposition Bias:
excessive disciplinary isolation / decomposition
→ context starvation / artificial boundaries / lost feedback loops
```

The research programme therefore follows the working principle:

> **preserve epistemic independence where needed; preserve transdisciplinary interaction where scientifically needed; make dependencies observable.**

Short form:

> **independence without isolation — interaction without authority laundering**

This is a research-design constraint, not a new canonical METHOD rule.

## Research question

Generate sufficiently independent and traceable evidence to determine:

- what was actually observed in the relevant success/failure/correction/open-framing fixtures;
- which mechanisms adequately explain those outcomes;
- which existing normative boundaries were effective, ineffective, mistimed or translated incorrectly;
- when local correctness did or did not compose into a globally adequate scholarly result;
- how disciplinary independence and transdisciplinary interaction affected evidence quality;
- which positive scholarly abilities appear in practice without presupposing that they are already generic system capabilities;
- what recurs across materially different source/project structures;
- what remains fixture-specific or uncertain.

## Research boundary

Research may explain, distinguish, compare and cautiously generalize.

Research does **not** yet decide:

- final generic capability map;
- new normative Requirements;
- Human/AI/software target allocation;
- workflow/role/agent architecture;
- candidate-question schema;
- semantic-handoff schema;
- state machine/data model;
- implementation.

A descriptive recurrence is not a normative promotion.

## Programme structure / dependencies

- #184 — **shared repository fixtures / competency interaction / Requirement efficacy**. May run independently of #185.
- #185 — **cross-source genericity / heterogeneous fixtures / external disciplinary evidence**. May run independently of #184.
- #186 — **Research integration / Concept Challenge**. Blocked on sufficiently frozen findings from both #184 and #185.

```text
existing audits + canonical Requirements + direct fixture evidence
        ↓
      #184 ───────────────┐
                         ├→ #186 integration / concept challenge
      #185 ───────────────┘
```

The integration stage must not retroactively rewrite primary findings to fit the current analysis model.

## Dependency provenance for material findings

Without imposing a new schema, research should retain enough provenance to reconstruct:

- evidence available to the judgement;
- already-known disciplinary findings;
- disciplinary interactions that occurred;
- premises inherited but not independently checked;
- claims independently checked;
- changes to question/evidence search/investigation direction caused by other competencies.

The purpose is to study the epistemic dependency structure of findings, not to enforce `independent=yes/no` or a fixed collaboration workflow.

## Completion condition

This programme is complete when the repository contains a decision-ready empirical evidence package that makes clear:

- observation vs interpretation vs hypothesis;
- alternative explanations;
- uncertainty/counterevidence;
- fixture/source/project-purpose limits;
- cross-competence vs cross-fixture vs cross-source recurrence;
- Requirement existence vs operative efficacy;
- interaction/dependency provenance where material;
- what the existing Concept Candidate is supported by, contradicted by, or simply lacks evidence for.

Completion of this Issue does not itself authorize Synthesis/Requirements promotion or implementation.
