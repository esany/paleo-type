## Governing requirement
The repository must make consequential research traceable, reproducible and critically inspectable in line with Good Research Practice, without turning documentation into a second project.

## Review criteria
Verify that a future structure supports:
- one canonical home for each durable fact/decision/state;
- recoverable provenance for sources, files, loci and research evidence;
- explicit distinction between inspected evidence, metadata, leads and inference;
- versioned decision/revision history for consequential changes;
- responsibility/provenance for consequential AI-assisted and human-reviewed work;
- disclosure of what has and has not received independent qualified review;
- preservation of rejected/ superseded readings where scientifically relevant;
- bounded corpus/search completeness claims;
- rights, access, confidentiality and licensing information where relevant;
- durable restartability without chat;
- sufficient documentation of methods/standards actually used and their material versions;
- clear retention of uncertainty and limitations rather than retrospective smoothing;
- reproducible derived outputs from canonical research data where feasible.

## Standards check
At review time re-check the then-current DFG Good Research Practice guidance and other applicable institutional/repository requirements. Translate only relevant principles into the smallest operational controls; do not treat compliance prose as a substitute for research quality.

## Measures if a gap is demonstrated
Possible minimal measures:
- repair canonical ownership and provenance paths;
- add responsibility/review metadata only where consequential;
- strengthen change/decision logging for scientifically material revisions;
- add rights/access fields where actual use requires them;
- add deterministic consistency/schema checks when the trigger in #11 is met;
- introduce structured bibliography only when #13 is triggered;
- add release/checkpoint packaging for reproducible research states when publication/freeze requires it;
- define retention/archive rules only when real volume or institutional obligations require them.

## Trigger
Review before publication/data release, a canonical-format migration, collaboration with additional researchers, transfer to institutional infrastructure, or when provenance/restartability failures recur.

## Definition of Done
- a third party can reconstruct consequential research state and evidence paths without chat;
- responsibilities and validation levels are not overstated;
- rights/access limits are visible where relevant;
- methods and material standard versions can be identified;
- rejected/superseded scientific claims remain traceable where needed;
- documentation burden is proportional to consequence and does not duplicate truth stores.

## Do not
Do not equate more metadata with better science. Do not add compliance fields that are never used to support traceability, integrity, rights or reproducibility.

### Concrete repo evidence

- `corpus/files.tsv` records filename, SHA-256, coverage, storage status, intended path, rights status and the explicit public-redistribution preservation blocker for all central exports.
- `methodology/maintenance.md` gives a clear one-fact/one-owner map and restart/consistency procedures.
- `apparatus/decisions.tsv` preserves rejected and superseded readings rather than smoothing them away.
- Project `STATUS.md` explicitly says no independent qualified palaeographic review of the full Knau transcription has been recorded.
- The exact human-readable witness is still missing, so reproducible derived-output requirements are not yet demonstrated end-to-end.

### Architecture handoff

Do not add generic compliance metadata. Later architecture should preserve the strong existing provenance model and add only the smallest responsibility/review representation needed to distinguish AI assistance, procedural checking and independent specialist validation. The vertical slice must prove a reproducible derived witness from canonical data. Rights/access remains human-authority input where external facts cannot be inferred.

Overall: stewardship/restartability are **strong**; the main demonstrated gaps are responsibility/review provenance at consequential scale and deterministic derived outputs.
