## Motivation from existing analysis

This workstream addresses the empirically thin parts of the current audit landscape, especially A1/A5/B1/B2/B4/B5, D2/D3 and the Level-2 distinction between Requirement existence and operative efficacy.

It also tests two competing risks without presupposing either:

- `Concept Bias` from pre-existing solution/analysis language;
- `Decomposition Bias` from treating disciplinary independence as isolation.

The unit of investigation is the **scientific problem/fixture**, not the competence family. Competencies are an available space activated only when materially implicated.

## Shared fixtures

Use the same repository cases across relevant perspectives so results can be compared without pretending that repeated analysis of one case is repeated empirical recurrence:

- historical G2 Domain Review as a success/adequacy fixture;
- #165/#171 as analysis-unit / premise-inheritance failure fixture;
- #169 as resource-state / task-authority correction fixture;
- post-#166 as open-framing fixture with rich evidence but no owner-selected next scope;
- #162 only as a freshness/derived-surface fixture if the stale-state condition is freshly verified;
- #90/main only as a limited control case for clear `requirement exists → enforcement missing` if freshly verified.

Do not modify #162 or #90 under this Issue.

## Primary research mode

Enable fachlich eigenständige judgements without assuming disciplinary isolation.

Problem-required interaction is allowed and is itself evidence. For material findings, retain enough dependency provenance to reconstruct:

- evidence available to the judgement;
- already-known disciplinary findings;
- interactions that occurred;
- inherited premises not independently checked;
- independently checked claims;
- changes in question/evidence search/investigation direction caused by other competencies.

Do not encode a fixed collaboration mode or `independent=yes/no` field.

## Primary questions

For each materially relevant perspective/interaction ask first, in domain-natural language:

- what is directly supported by the evidence?
- what fachlich relevant process, problem, success or boundary is visible?
- what alternative explanations exist?
- what could not legitimately be concluded?
- what would have changed the judgement?
- what is fixture-bound vs potentially transferable?

Only after those primary findings are frozen, test secondary hypotheses such as:

- activation/timing/sequence failure;
- visibility/freshness/translation problem;
- local vs interaction-level failure;
- protective guardrail vs productive scholarly ability;
- `earliest detectable`, `earliest preventable`, `earliest recoverable` point, including `no earlier prevention justified by available evidence`.

## Requirement efficacy

For existing relevant Requirements, investigate rather than assume:

```text
exists?
→ trigger recognizable?
→ information visible?
→ relevant competence available?
→ activated at an appropriate time?
→ output preserved/translated correctly?
→ correct consumer/consequence?
→ review/enforcement?
→ globally adequate result?
```

A locally correct Requirement/mechanism may still compose into an inadequate result; equally, apparent composition failure may reduce to a concrete faulty translation. Keep both explanations open.

## Evidence outputs

Persist primary findings before later integration. Each material finding should distinguish at minimum:

- direct observation/evidence;
- interpretation;
- alternative explanation;
- uncertainty;
- tentative scope/generalisation;
- relevant dependency provenance.

Do not force technical observability, deterministic-check or AI-allocation fields into the primary record.

## Completion criteria

- [ ] relevant shared fixtures are freshly reconstructed from canonical/direct evidence;
- [ ] competency activation is problem-driven rather than list-driven;
- [ ] `not materially applicable` and `requires transdisciplinary interaction` are both valid outcomes;
- [ ] primary findings are frozen before secondary gap classification;
- [ ] Requirement existence is separated from Requirement efficacy;
- [ ] independent judgement is separated from isolation;
- [ ] cross-competence convergence on one fixture is not counted as cross-fixture recurrence;
- [ ] uncertainty and counter-explanations remain visible;
- [ ] findings are persisted for later integration under #183.
