## Required semantics now in force

When a PDF, scan, attachment, credential, tool capability or other resource is unavailable:

```text
RESOURCE UNAVAILABLE: <resource>
AFFECTED ACTION: <specific action that cannot currently execute>
OBSERVED BASIS: <what was actually tested/seen>
```

Before promoting that fact to `BLOCKED`, `STOP`, changed `NEXT ACTION`, task-wide prerequisite or roadmap dependency, execution must identify:

```text
DEPENDENCY AUTHORITY: <canonical task/Project owner or explicit Human-Owner decision>
DEPENDENCY: <resource X is prerequisite for action/task Y>
AUTHORIZED CONSEQUENCE: <only what that authority actually establishes>
```

If that authority is absent or ambiguous, preserve only the resource fact + affected-action limitation and surface any material planning ambiguity to the Human Owner.

Resource absence does **not** relax evidence standards. A source-dependent action that needs unavailable pixels still cannot be simulated from metadata, prior transcription or another witness.
