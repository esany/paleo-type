## Problem

A fresh lifecycle audit found that the generic method already distinguishes representation from resolution and activities from maturity gates, but human-facing Project state could still stack labels such as `G2 PASS`, `#98 COMPLETE`, `#99 COMPLETE` in a way that suggested a finished diplomatic text/edition.

The concrete Reg. Ii 4 Knau case demonstrates the ambiguity: all 117 relevant visible units of fol. 306v–309r are represented and internal analysis was completed, while many readings/sign systems/overwritten passages remain explicitly unresolved. The historical #99 comparison was valid bounded collation work, but Reg. Ii 5 still lacks a current independent canonical diplomatic witness representation.

## Changes

- make every G1–G6 claim explicitly scope/locus-bound;
- distinguish work-package completion, representational completeness, reading resolution, gate attainment and edition production;
- align G2 writing-characteristic/possible-hand-shift semantics with current transcription guidelines;
- require Project STATUS to expose evaluated scope and residual limits for gate claims;
- Reg. Ii 4 Knau lifecycle now states explicitly:
  - G1 reached for the bounded investigation;
  - G2 reached as representational completeness for fol. 306v–309r;
  - reading resolution still incomplete with explicit residual uncertainty;
  - G3 reached at current internal-evidence level;
  - #99 work package complete but G4 not reached;
  - G5/G6 not reached;
- Reg. Ii 5 fol. 61v–63r now states:
  - G1 reached;
  - #115 active;
  - G2 active/not reached;
  - G3–G6 not reached;
- Reg. Ii 3 now explicitly states that Source/File/crosswalk readiness does not activate or imply an edition-gate progression in the absence of an owner-selected bounded target;
- qualify Reg. Ii 4 project/use/evidence wording so historical task completion cannot be read as full reading/edition completion.

## Non-changes

- no manuscript reading changes;
- no source relationship changes;
- no new gate requirements beyond clarifying existing semantics;
- no reopening of #96–#99;
- no new current Reg. Ii 4 target;
- no change to #115 witness-independence rules;
- no schema/model migration.

This is a restartability and scholarly-status semantics correction prompted by the concrete observation that `complete` was being used at several different lifecycle levels.
