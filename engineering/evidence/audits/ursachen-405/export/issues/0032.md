## Governing requirement
Material/codicological structure must be represented when it affects dating, transmission, document structure, reading order or interpretation, while remaining explicitly bounded when digital images cannot support the claim.

## Review criteria
Verify that the architecture can, when relevant, distinguish and document:
- support/material and format;
- foliation/pagination systems and anomalies;
- quires/gatherings, binding and attachments where observable;
- watermarks, ruling, writing areas and material construction where consequential;
- damage, loss, repairs, cropping and other physical conditions affecting text;
- relations among physical object, record/document unit and digital instantiation;
- limits of what can be inferred from scans/exports versus physical inspection;
- `not investigated`, `not observable`, `not relevant` and `not present` as different states;
- material evidence separately from palaeographic or archival inference.

## Experiential-knowledge check
Expert codicological experience may identify diagnostic features or questions, but the system must expose whether the feature was actually visible in the available representation and whether physical inspection would be required.

## Measures if a gap is demonstrated
Possible minimal measures:
- add project-level material/document observations only for observed consequential features;
- extend source/instantiation provenance when cropping/scaling/rotation obscures material evidence;
- represent structural boundaries/attachments where linear transcription loses meaning;
- create reusable codicological fields only after repeated cross-project need;
- require targeted physical-archive or expert inspection when digital evidence is insufficient and the claim is consequential.

## Trigger
Review when material structure affects text order/boundaries, dating/transmission claims, physical damage creates major uncertainty, new manuscript types require codicological description, or publication makes material claims consequential.

## Definition of Done
- material claims are recoverable to observed evidence or explicitly marked as unobservable/not investigated;
- physical structure is not silently inferred from digital export order;
- source representation preserves material relations that affect meaning;
- the data model remains lean where material features are irrelevant;
- specialist review/physical inspection requirements are explicit when digital evidence cannot decide.

## Do not
Do not add a full manuscript-description schema to every project by default. Do not infer physical codex structure from filenames, page sequence or catalogue expectations alone.
