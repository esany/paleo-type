## Result

Generator B is problem-type dependent. It gives useful candidate material on `62v-L11 steinbruch` and fragment-level help on unresolved `62r-L09` and `62r-L23`; it also localizes uncertainty at `62r-L19` but does not supply the missing `So` alternative. It fails materially on `62r-L20 gemeinswalde` and produces a fluent wrong candidate at `62r-L06`.

Decision: **KEEP CONDITIONALLY as a bounded Generator-B source for the next A-vs-B comparison; do not integrate into the default packet and do not build broader HTR infrastructure.**

The record explicitly documents the execution-environment workaround, setup burden, cold/warm timing boundary, failure character, and the limitation that procedural ordering here is not equivalent to independent cognitive blinding.

No canonical reading, STATUS, project_context contract, METHOD, HTR training architecture, or A+B fusion is changed.
