## Kommentar 5651747369 · 2026-09-13T06:47:28Z · esany

## Concept clarification at pause — passive bulletin-board model

This note preserves the Human Owner's clarified design intent while the #162 pilot remains paused. It is **conceptual evidence only**: it does not resume the pilot, admit an implementation, change METHOD/schema, or alter canonical research/project authority.

### Core model

The coordination channel is not a second workspace, governance layer, or decision surface. It is a **passive delivery/bulletin-board layer** ("Wandzeitung" / newspaper / mailbox):

- it may deliver bounded requests, replies, notices and completion acknowledgements;
- it may expose enough routing/status metadata to make delivery deterministic and inspectable;
- it may mechanically mark or correlate `sent` / `received` / `answered` / thread relationships if such mechanics are later admitted;
- it must **not** choose priorities, create/alter scope, fabricate `NEXT ACTION`, accept reviews, resolve scholarly disagreements, or treat transport history as canonical state;
- any durable/consequential outcome still returns through the applicable repository/Issue/PR/Project owner and existing authority/review path.

In short: **the channel may transport and mechanically account for messages; it may not govern the work.**

### Human control surface vs canonical authority vs transport

The intended separation is:

```text
ChatGPT chat(s)
= primary human-visible work/control interface
  - local analysis, argumentation, questions, explanations
  - Human Owner can inspect/intervene directly
  - not canonical research-state authority and not historical source evidence

GitHub / canonical repository owners
= durable authority/memory
  - scope/priority where owned there
  - accepted decisions and review consequences
  - canonical research/method/project state

Slack coordination channel
= passive transport / bulletin board
  - delivery and visibility only
  - no independent project/research authority
```

The Human Owner therefore primarily steers work in the actual ChatGPT contexts. If an instruction or decision becomes durable/consequential, it must be persisted in the appropriate canonical owner before a later context relies on it. The channel does not interpret the Human Owner's steering into new authority.

### Two-part message concept

A useful future packet may have two deliberately different layers:

1. **Machine block** — optimized for deterministic routing/correlation and allowed to be maximally compact once semantics are stable. It should contain pointers/transport metadata rather than copied research truth, e.g. message ID, target context/role, repository, task/Issue owner pointer, sender checkpoint hint, message kind, reply/thread pointer/status.
2. **Human block** — concise, ordinary-language explanation readable without decoding. It should make clear who needs what from whom, the task/repo context, what action/return is requested, and where any durable consequence belongs.

The machine block routes; the human block explains. **Neither is a canonical truth store.** Stable Project/Source/File/task facts remain freshly derived from their owners rather than duplicated into transport packets.

Illustrative shape only (not an admitted schema):

```text
[MACHINE]
v=1
id=...
to=review
repo=esany/paleo-type
owner=issue:162
basis=main@...
kind=request
reply_to=thread
[/MACHINE]

[HUMAN]
Please review X against the current repository/task state.
Persist any durable consequence in the applicable GitHub/repository owner.
[/HUMAN]
```

A later machine serialization may be aggressively compact if that improves reliability/cost, but this should not be frozen before real semantics are stable and should never become a parallel ontology/state model.

### Guardrail implied by the model

The previously identified staleness/authority-laundering risk remains central. A receiver may use the machine block to find the relevant repo/task, but must re-resolve the **applicable current canonical owner state for the concrete request** before consequential action. A sender SHA or transport status is routing/comparison metadata, not authority.

### Human-auditability criterion

Success is not merely `messages can move without copy/paste`. The model is acceptable only if the Human Owner can continue to understand and steer the actual work through the normal ChatGPT conversations without having to supervise a second decision-making system. Ideally the channel is rarely opened by the owner; it behaves like infrastructure/post, while the chats remain the visible work/control surface and GitHub remains the durable authority surface.

Pause remains in force. No implementation or further coordination experiment is authorized by this note.
