## RF184-P01 — G2 could classify established structure without forcing unresolved readings

**Fixture:** G2 Domain Review Checkpoint B.

### Direct observation / evidence

The G2 review repeatedly separates an established structural/source phenomenon from unresolved wording or interpretation. Examples include:

- eight physical list/accounting lines are accepted while W1–W6 readings/values remain unresolved;
- spatial alignment exists while individual sign values remain unresolved;
- cancellation exists while deleted wording/replacement relation remains unresolved;
- written Arabic `80` is preserved as a sign while its value/unit/function remains unresolved;
- exact row-to-physical-line mapping can remain ambiguous without turning the ambiguity itself into a new system requirement;
- decision-history semantics can be judged adequate even while target precision remains coarse.

The review explicitly asks whether the scholarly information itself is established before deciding whether a representational/system consequence follows.

### Interpretation

A useful scholarly operation in this fixture was the ability to **stop at the evidentially established distinction** rather than require all adjacent uncertainty to be resolved before classification. This allowed structure, phenomenon, addressing and history questions to be treated differently from unresolved palaeographic/metrological readings.

### Alternative explanation

The result could be attributed mainly to unusually rich, already-structured Reg. Ii 5 evidence rather than to a broadly useful transdisciplinary review function. Cross-source evidence is required before treating the operation as generic.

### Uncertainty / evidence ceiling

This fixture demonstrates a successful operation, but not that every research domain can or should use the same classification path.

### Tentative transferability

`single-fixture success / cross-domain convergence within one fixture`.

### Dependency provenance

The review depended on prior direct Source Research, existing Requirements and canonical Project/source records. It did not establish the underlying readings independently; it classified consequences from already-bounded findings.

## RF184-P02 — G2 distinguished an evidence gap from a representation/system gap

**Fixture:** G2 Domain Review Checkpoint B.

### Direct observation / evidence

The review explicitly rejects the rule `missing legacy representation = system defect`. It requires established scholarly information, demonstrated loss in current canonical support and reusable/material need before admitting a deterministic-system candidate.

Conversely, where row-level equivalence or deleted wording remained unresolved, the result was `needs further evidence` or continued research rather than a Dev conclusion.

### Interpretation

The positive function demonstrated here is not merely a guardrail against overbuilding. It is an ability to decide **what kind of uncertainty exists** and therefore what kind of next action is scientifically admissible: continue research, preserve existing adequate support, or consider bounded system support.

### Alternative explanation

This may be specific to representation/migration questions and not directly transferable to pre-task research-question selection.

### Uncertainty / evidence ceiling

No evidence from this fixture alone shows that the same operation generates or prioritizes new research questions.

### Tentative transferability

`single-fixture success; plausible relevance to Domain↔Dev boundary; no generic research-framing conclusion yet`.

### Dependency provenance

The judgement uses multiple disciplinary requirements plus existing canonical representation state. It is therefore neither a monodisciplinary observation nor a purely technical decision.

## RF184-P03 — #165 overcorrection promoted a comparison relation into source-side unit authority

**Fixture:** #165 / corrected source-understanding state.

### Direct observation / evidence

The persisted correction history states that the first #165 execution correctly challenged an earlier assumption that `62r-L19–25` and `62v-L01–07` were already independent functional units, but then promoted the larger G4 correspondence range `62r-L19–62v-L07` into one cross-folio Reg. Ii 5 source/text unit.

Direct Reg. Ii 5 source inspection had two adjacent paragraph blocks. The controlled Reg. Ii 4 witness also represented the corresponding material as two separately initialled regions. Later #166 source inspection established two distinct adjacent rules/decrees.

### Interpretation

The minimum sufficient explanation is a **semantic/evidential translation error**: a valid comparison/correspondence relation acquired stronger source-structure authority than its evidence warranted.

The fixture does not require an emergent multi-component failure explanation to explain the core error.

### Alternative explanation

Broader interaction effects may have amplified the error: higher-level synthesis had recently produced real gains, G4 carried a convenient larger range, and later restart/review surfaces could stabilize the derived premise. These factors may explain persistence even if the originating error was a concrete translation mistake.

### Uncertainty / evidence ceiling

The repository history does not by itself quantify how much each amplifying factor contributed.

### Tentative transferability

`repeated within this Project as a boundary-risk pattern; genericity unproven`.

### Dependency provenance

The erroneous unit claim depended on a previously established G4 mapping and an inherited assumption about analysis scale. The later correction descended to the direct source inventory and controlled comparator structure.

The fixtures reject a simple `isolated = independent` model.

### G2

### #169

The core problem occurs at an interface between evidence/resource semantics and task/Project authority. No single discipline needs to be isolated; what matters is preserving the authority boundary during consequence formation.

### Current bounded inference

The internal fixtures support the Research principle:

> preserve independent checking where the claim requires it, but do not remove scientifically necessary cross-domain interaction; record dependency provenance so premise inheritance can be distinguished from legitimate sequential dependence.

This remains a Research observation, not a workflow prescription.
