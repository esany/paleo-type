## RI186-03 — analytical scale is question/evidence dependent across heterogeneous source structures

### Upstream evidence

#184/#165 shows that a valid correspondence range must not automatically become the source-side analysis unit.

#185 shows:

- Darwin Letter 5098 remains an individual source object while dating can require a correspondence relation beyond the page;
- Washington cash-account entries remain addressable while economic/transactional questions can require aggregation across entries or an account period;
- Reg. Ii 5 physical addresses, paragraph blocks, correspondence ranges and functional rules can diverge.

### Integrated finding

The strongest cross-source descriptive proposition is not `larger unit = better analysis` and not `smallest unit = safest analysis`.

It is:

> **Stable source address, documentary/source object, evidence relation and useful analysis unit are distinct, and the appropriate analytical scale depends on the question and available evidence.**

### Evidence status

`cross-source recurrence / provisional generic hypothesis`.

### Research consequence

This is stronger evidence for an adaptive analytical practice than for any universal analysis-unit object model.

## RI186-04 — context timing is claim-dependent, not uniformly early or late

### Upstream evidence

#185 compares:

- Reg. Ii 5: comparator/context wording can contaminate direct reading and is therefore deliberately withheld during a sensitive stage, then reintroduced for controlled comparison;
- Darwin correspondence: related letters, archival provenance and other contextual clues can be constitutive evidence for dating/identification;
- Washington accounts: aggregate/accounting context can be required to establish transactional/economic meaning.

### Integrated finding

The evidence rejects a universal sequence such as:

```text
source-only first for every claim
→ context later
```

and equally rejects unrestricted context-first interpretation.

A better descriptive proposition is:

> **The legitimacy and timing of context depend on the claim being established and on whether the context is direct/relational evidence, hypothesis-generating context or a contamination risk.**

### Evidence status

`cross-source recurrence / provisional generic hypothesis`.

### Research consequence

Any future workflow or context mechanism that imposes one fixed stage boundary across all claim types would be over-specified relative to current evidence.

## RI186-05 — epistemic independence is not organizational isolation

### Upstream evidence

#184/#171 shows that a fresh/re-hydrated review can still require deeper direct-evidence checking of the disputed premise.

#185 shows:

- Darwin dating can responsibly depend on related letters and provenance evidence;
- account interpretation can require aggregation while individual entries/amounts remain independently checked;
- Reg. Ii 5 direct grapheme claims may need insulation from semantic comparator wording.

### Integrated finding

A more adequate descriptive model is:

> **Independence concerns which evidence and premises were independently tested at the required depth, not whether the judgement occurred without interaction.**

Transdisciplinary interaction can be legitimate or constitutive while some upstream claims remain independently controlled.

### Evidence status

`cross-fixture + cross-source recurrence / provisional generic Research finding`.

### Research consequence

The current Research principle remains supported:

> **independence without isolation — interaction without authority laundering**

but it remains a Research-design finding, not a fixed workflow topology.

`cross-source recurrence / provisional generic hypothesis`.

## RI186-07 — the most visible positive practice is controlled scale/context switching, not a single demonstrated framing procedure

### Upstream evidence

Across the fixtures, useful research can require:

- withholding semantic context for a contamination-sensitive reading;
- reactivating related letters/provenance to establish chronology;
- aggregating account entries to expose transactional meaning;
- returning from an over-broad comparison range to direct source structure;
- preserving unresolved detail while accepting established structure/function;
- stopping at a bounded evidence ceiling instead of manufacturing a backlog.

### Integrated finding

The strongest current positive evidence is for an **adaptive scholarly ability to change analytical scale, evidence depth and context exposure in response to the question and evidential state**.

This is not yet evidence that the generic system should encode one capability with that name.

### Evidence status

`cross-source provisional scholarly-practice finding`.

### Research consequence

Later synthesis should be cautious about representing productive research as one linear pre-execution layer.

# Part C — dependency and transdisciplinarity findings

## 5. Observable dependency patterns

The current evidence supports retaining dependency provenance because materially different epistemic patterns occur:

### Independent upstream claim checking

Example: direct visual/source claim checked without allowing semantic plausibility to manufacture the observation.

### Legitimate sequential dependency

Example: lexical/metrological or historical interpretation depends on a sufficiently supported reading/sign claim.

### Relational evidence dependence

Example: Darwin dating depends on other correspondence/provenance evidence rather than the isolated letter alone.

### Aggregate evidence dependence

Example: account interpretation may require transaction/account-level structure beyond an isolated entry.

### Productive joint classification

Example: G2 combines domain requirements and representation state to classify consequences without one competence owning all underlying facts.

### Premise inheritance

Example: #165/#171 shows how a derived larger-unit premise can persist until review descends far enough into the direct evidence hierarchy.

### Authority-laundering transition

Example: #169 shows a valid resource fact gaining task/roadmap consequence without sufficient authority.

## 6. What is not established

The evidence does not justify a fixed set of collaboration modes, a permanent lead/contribute/review matrix or a universal dependency graph.

The more defensible Research statement is:

> Different questions require different dependency structures; those dependencies should remain inspectable enough to distinguish independent checking, legitimate dependence and unexamined premise inheritance.

## 8. Strongly retained portions of the Concept Candidate

The Research gives comparatively strong support to retaining the following as **later-design constraints/hypotheses**, not yet normative additions:

- evidence/authority boundaries must survive translation;
- Project purpose/priority authority remains Human-Owner controlled;
- source address and analytical object/scale must not be collapsed;
- technical PASS/enforcement is separate from scholarly adequacy;
- canonical/derived/current/historical distinctions matter;
- deterministic enforcement is appropriate where semantics are already stable and admitted;
- competence relevance varies by claim/question/source;
- cross-source validation is necessary before generic system promotion;
- scholarly dependencies should be inspectable enough to distinguish independent checking from inherited premises.

Several of these already have canonical normative owners; Research support is not a reason to duplicate them.

The Research most clearly challenges the following degrees of specificity:

- one linear `research-framing layer` as the presumed form of pre-task scholarship;
- one candidate-question object/field set;
- one permanent or semi-fixed claim→lead competence table;
- one universal semantic-handoff contract format;
- fixed Human/AI/software allocation before empirical function analysis;
- treating all illustrative invariants as equally machine-enforceable;
- treating the complete interface-literacy list as already demonstrated;
- equating independent analysis with isolated competence runs;
- using `earliest preventable point` without detectability/recoverability/hindsight controls.

De-specification does not mean rejection. It means return to the lower-level evidenced need before comparing solution forms.

# Part E — what is now decision-ready versus still open

## 11. Research conclusions that are sufficiently supported for later decision-making

A later Synthesis/Requirements phase can now safely use the following as **Research inputs**:

1. The current problem landscape contains multiple distinct mechanisms; one catch-all root cause is not supported.
2. Requirement existence and Requirement efficacy are materially different questions, but efficacy itself must be decomposed by mechanism.
3. Source address/documentary object/evidence relation/analysis unit must remain distinguishable; analytical scale is question/evidence dependent.
4. Context timing is claim-dependent; one fixed context-isolation/reactivation sequence is not supported across source types.
5. Epistemic independence is about evidence/premise checking, not absence of interaction.
6. Transdisciplinary interaction can be constitutive during inquiry for some claims.
7. Controlled scale/context switching and evidence-bounded stopping are empirically more visible productive practices than one generic question-generation procedure.
8. Actual research-question formation is materially source-/purpose-sensitive.
9. Repository/governance support failures must remain analytically distinct from source-scholarship failures unless analogous evidence connects them.
10. The Concept Candidate contains both well-supported boundary ideas and solution detail that currently outruns evidence.

These are still Research findings, not Requirements.

## 13. Remaining evidence gaps before a high-confidence holistic solution synthesis

The most valuable remaining empirical work would be narrower than the earlier broad concept programme.

### 13.1 Qualified competency / specialist evidence

#184/#185 are AI-assisted Research, not independent qualified specialist validation.

A later high-confidence synthesis would benefit from actual disciplinary challenge on the claims where competence boundaries matter most, especially:

- question formation / historical method;
- context timing in source criticism/editorial practice;
- analysis-unit formation in diplomatics/codicology/source criticism;
- accounting/metrological interpretation boundaries;
- evidence-depth requirements in consequential review.

This need not become one run per discipline; the scientific problem should determine participation.

### 13.2 Empirical question-generation comparison

The largest conceptual gap remains that no fixture has yet **demonstrated and compared several serious next research questions** from the same rich state.

A bounded Research experiment could compare how different fachlich grounded approaches formulate questions from:

- post-#166 Reg. Ii 5 state;
- a correspondence fixture;
- an accounting fixture;

without automatically authorizing any resulting scope.

### 13.3 Human-Owner evidence

HCI claims remain literature-/repository-/scenario-level only.

Actual Owner observation would be required before claiming that any proposed decision surface, amount of information or comparison format works empirically.

### 13.4 AI/context experiment

Concept Bias, premise inheritance and context-order effects remain plausible but not experimentally measured in this programme.

A later bounded experiment could compare outputs under controlled evidence/context compositions without turning the experiment into a permanent agent architecture.

### 13.5 Formalizability / enforcement analysis

Known technical cases such as #90 and #162 show that some gaps are suitable for deterministic support, but a systematic analysis of which semantic distinctions are safe to encode remains incomplete.

The correct next question there is property-by-property, not `software control plane yes/no`.

## 14. Synthesis-readiness judgement

The Research programme has materially improved the evidence base enough to make a later **Requirements/Capability decision phase possible**, but not enough to make one predetermined holistic architecture trustworthy.

A later phase can now reason from evidence rather than the Concept Candidate alone.

However, if the intended next step is a **high-confidence generic architecture** rather than a bounded Requirements decision, the remaining specialist, HCI, AI-context and formalizability evidence above should be weighed before locking design form.

This is intentionally not a recommendation to start that phase automatically. Human-Owner admission remains required.
